## Creative constraints

- Human POV only, defaulting to close third person through Nera Brindle.
- Human conflict first: war, debt, hunger, roads, witness, cowardice, family, and survival.
- Aurelith presence stays thin-veil: Starvel Field, white stones, bad maps, taboo, and rare blue-white distance.
- No Aurelith POV, village administration, direct explanation, chosen child, rebellion epic, or world-saving plot.
- Solve weak chapters by sharpening choices and cutting clutter, not by expanding lore.

## Human-first rule

Every scene must be driven by something a human character wants, fears, hides, owes, needs, or risks. The Aurelith layer can shape the landscape and the customs around it, but it should not solve the plot.

## Thin-veil Aurelith rule

Starvel Field should remain mostly level 1–2 on the contact scale: rumor, taboo, boundary, road bend, white stones, and possible distant light. No elf needs to appear on page.
