# Low Wicker — Description

## Type of Place
damp sheep valley / village.

## First Appearance
Chapter 1 - Levy Bell — `book-one/manuscript/chapter-01-levy-bell.md`.

## Later Appearances
all chapters planned.

## Physical Description
A low rural valley of sheep folds, market mud, smoke, rain, small houses, toll traffic, and a village that counts winter in sacks of grain.

## Weather / Seasonal Feel
Damp, rain-prone, cold at the edges; mud appears faster than dignity.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Wet wool, barley bread, smoke, sheep, iron bells, sour ale, hoof-churned road mud.

## Human Use
People bake, barter, count debts, read rolls, hide children, gossip, and decide what truth costs.

## Political Control
Nominal local reeve authority under Lord Venn’s road pressure and larger human border claims.

## Social Meaning
Home, trap, witness crowd, and the place whose survival can be used against its children.

## Local Customs
Bridge tolls, bread debts, chapel witness, lowered banners near Starvel Field.

## Dangers
Levy, hunger, debt, informants, searches, reprisal.

## Secrets
Who knew the levy roll was bent, who benefits from the false fire, who will speak when watched.

Nera loves it as home and resents how well it has trained her to be quiet.
