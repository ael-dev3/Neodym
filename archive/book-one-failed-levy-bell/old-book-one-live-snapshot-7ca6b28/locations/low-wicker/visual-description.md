# Low Wicker — Visual Description

## Core Visual Identity
A low rural valley of sheep folds, market mud, smoke, rain, small houses, toll traffic, and a village that counts winter in sacks of grain.

## Shapes and Layout
Keep the layout readable as a human working place: roads, doors, thresholds, storage, fields, or stones should imply what people do there.

## Materials
Wool, mud, wet stone, road dust, old wood, iron, grain, ash, and cheap leather as appropriate.

## Weather / Light
Damp, rain-prone, cold at the edges; mud appears faster than dignity.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Wet wool, barley bread, smoke, sheep, iron bells, sour ale, hoof-churned road mud.

## Human Activity
People bake, barter, count debts, read rolls, hide children, gossip, and decide what truth costs.

## Color Palette
Grounded rural colors: mud brown, rain gray, barley gold, ash black, wool cream, wet stone.

## Symbolic Motif
mud and bells under low rain

## Image Generation Prompt
Grounded high-fantasy rural location concept art of Low Wicker, damp sheep valley / village, in the world of Neodym Book One, human-first, tactile mud and wool realism, restrained palette, rain and road pressure, no modern technology, no generic elven city, no lore-explainer composition.

## Negative Prompt
No sci-fi screens, no neon, no clean castle fantasy gloss, no elves explaining the scene, no massive battle, no modern roads, no franchise imitation.
