# Old Road Around Starvel Field — Visual Description

## Core Visual Identity
Narrower old route bending around white stones and bad ground, half-kept by memory more than maps.

## Shapes and Layout
Keep the layout readable as a human working place: roads, doors, thresholds, storage, fields, or stones should imply what people do there.

## Materials
Wool, mud, wet stone, road dust, old wood, iron, grain, ash, and cheap leather as appropriate.

## Weather / Light
Rain makes it slick; mist makes distances uncertain.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Thorn, wet grass, stone under mud, horse breath, quiet beyond the stones.

## Human Activity
Used by those avoiding watched roads, old chapel traffic, smugglers, and desperate witnesses.

## Color Palette
Grounded rural colors: mud brown, rain gray, barley gold, ash black, wool cream, wet stone.

## Symbolic Motif
road bending around white stones

## Image Generation Prompt
Grounded high-fantasy rural location concept art of Old Road Around Starvel Field, older chapel way / boundary-skirt road, in the world of Neodym Book One, human-first, tactile mud and wool realism, restrained palette, rain and road pressure, no modern technology, no generic elven city, no lore-explainer composition.

## Negative Prompt
No sci-fi screens, no neon, no clean castle fantasy gloss, no elves explaining the scene, no massive battle, no modern roads, no franchise imitation.
