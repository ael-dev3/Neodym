# The Split Loaf — Description

## Type of Place
bakehouse, inn, gossip court, shelter.

## First Appearance
Chapter 1 - Levy Bell — `book-one/manuscript/chapter-01-levy-bell.md`.

## Later Appearances
Chapters 2, 3, 5, 11, 12 planned.

## Physical Description
Low beams, hot ovens, flour-dust rafters, cooling boards, yard wall, shutters ticking with rain.

## Weather / Seasonal Feel
Safe heat inside; wet mud and horses outside.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Barley loaves, smoke, yeast sourness, oven stone, flour on skin, wet boots at the door.

## Human Use
People buy bread, hide news, argue over risks, and find temporary shelter.

## Political Control
Owned by Osa Fen but vulnerable to license, search, debt, and lord pressure.

## Social Meaning
The unofficial heart of practical mercy in Low Wicker.

## Local Customs
Blessing cuts on loaves, bread debts, quiet shelter for those with nowhere else.

## Dangers
Searches, accusations of hiding Pell, closure or fines.

## Secrets
Hidden rolls, hidden children, unpaid debts, who receives bread without coin.
