# The Split Loaf — Visual Description

## Core Visual Identity
Low beams, hot ovens, flour-dust rafters, cooling boards, yard wall, shutters ticking with rain.

## Shapes and Layout
Keep the layout readable as a human working place: roads, doors, thresholds, storage, fields, or stones should imply what people do there.

## Materials
Wool, mud, wet stone, road dust, old wood, iron, grain, ash, and cheap leather as appropriate.

## Weather / Light
Safe heat inside; wet mud and horses outside.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Barley loaves, smoke, yeast sourness, oven stone, flour on skin, wet boots at the door.

## Human Activity
People buy bread, hide news, argue over risks, and find temporary shelter.

## Color Palette
Grounded rural colors: mud brown, rain gray, barley gold, ash black, wool cream, wet stone.

## Symbolic Motif
oven stones and flour-white arms

## Image Generation Prompt
Grounded high-fantasy rural location concept art of The Split Loaf, bakehouse, inn, gossip court, shelter, in the world of Neodym Book One, human-first, tactile mud and wool realism, restrained palette, rain and road pressure, no modern technology, no generic elven city, no lore-explainer composition.

## Negative Prompt
No sci-fi screens, no neon, no clean castle fantasy gloss, no elves explaining the scene, no massive battle, no modern roads, no franchise imitation.
