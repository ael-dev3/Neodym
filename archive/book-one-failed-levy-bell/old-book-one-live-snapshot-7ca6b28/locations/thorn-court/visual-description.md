# Thorn Court — Visual Description

## Core Visual Identity
A small court place with old benches, thorn hedges or oath-thorns, frightened clerks, and little grandeur.

## Shapes and Layout
Keep the layout readable as a human working place: roads, doors, thresholds, storage, fields, or stones should imply what people do there.

## Materials
Wool, mud, wet stone, road dust, old wood, iron, grain, ash, and cheap leather as appropriate.

## Weather / Light
Damp, crowded, and sour with delayed business.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Wet wool, old ink, ale breath, hedge leaves, scraped benches.

## Human Activity
Hears small disputes and could certify a letter if not bought or frightened.

## Color Palette
Grounded rural colors: mud brown, rain gray, barley gold, ash black, wool cream, wet stone.

## Symbolic Motif
thorn hedge around a frightened court

## Image Generation Prompt
Grounded high-fantasy rural location concept art of Thorn Court, minor human court / witness stop, in the world of Neodym Book One, human-first, tactile mud and wool realism, restrained palette, rain and road pressure, no modern technology, no generic elven city, no lore-explainer composition.

## Negative Prompt
No sci-fi screens, no neon, no clean castle fantasy gloss, no elves explaining the scene, no massive battle, no modern roads, no franchise imitation.
