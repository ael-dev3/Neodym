# Saint Orra's Chapel — Description

## Type of Place
small stone chapel and witness place.

## First Appearance
Chapter 4 planned — `book-one/chapter-guides/chapter-04-saint-orras-west-door.md`.

## Later Appearances
Chapters 8, 10, 12 planned.

## Physical Description
Small stone building, west door, poor burial ground, candle racks, locked chests, old witness marks.

## Weather / Seasonal Feel
Cold stone and damp threshold, with candle heat that never reaches the corners.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Wax, old wool, wet stone, dust in registers, iron keys.

## Human Use
People swear oaths, bury poor dead, ask for quiet bargains, and seek forms power might respect.

## Political Control
Sister Auma keeps keys; nobles can ignore it unless public custom makes that costly.

## Social Meaning
Weak shelter for truth and memory.

## Local Customs
West-door witness, certified letters, burial fees, road blessings.

## Dangers
Institutional retaliation, ignored testimony, false comfort.

## Secrets
Old routes, old customs, possibly half-preserved witness records.
