# Toll Bridge — Visual Description

## Core Visual Identity
Practical bridge with toll marks, a bell/tower, wet boards or stone, and road traffic squeezed into one argument.

## Shapes and Layout
Keep the layout readable as a human working place: roads, doors, thresholds, storage, fields, or stones should imply what people do there.

## Materials
Wool, mud, wet stone, road dust, old wood, iron, grain, ash, and cheap leather as appropriate.

## Weather / Light
Slippery in rain, loud with hooves and sheep, cold over the water.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Iron bell, river damp, hoof mud, toll chalk, leather harness.

## Human Activity
People cross, pay, argue, count sheep, move grain, and hear public orders.

## Color Palette
Grounded rural colors: mud brown, rain gray, barley gold, ash black, wool cream, wet stone.

## Symbolic Motif
iron bell and damp toll marks

## Image Generation Prompt
Grounded high-fantasy rural location concept art of Toll Bridge, human toll bridge and road choke point, in the world of Neodym Book One, human-first, tactile mud and wool realism, restrained palette, rain and road pressure, no modern technology, no generic elven city, no lore-explainer composition.

## Negative Prompt
No sci-fi screens, no neon, no clean castle fantasy gloss, no elves explaining the scene, no massive battle, no modern roads, no franchise imitation.
