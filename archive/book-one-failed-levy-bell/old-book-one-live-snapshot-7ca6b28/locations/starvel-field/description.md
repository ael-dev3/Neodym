# Starvel Field — Description

## Type of Place
forbidden upland / Aurelith-adjacent boundary.

## First Appearance
Chapter 1 - Levy Bell — `book-one/manuscript/chapter-01-levy-bell.md`.

## Later Appearances
Chapters 6, 10, 12 planned.

## Physical Description
Pale field above the old road with white stones along the lower edge; maps bend around it.

## Weather / Seasonal Feel
Often seen through rain, mist, or far blue-white weather; never explained.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Quiet too large for the valley, horses balking, wind changed by distance, wet grass no one cuts.

## Human Use
People avoid it, lower banners near it, argue about it, and use its taboo for human politics.

## Political Control
No local human truly controls it; custom and fear control behavior around it.

## Social Meaning
A boundary that makes human banners look temporary.

## Local Customs
No banner raised near the stones; armies route around it.

## Dangers
Taboo, military hesitation, panic, social accusation, possible uncanny risk.

## Secrets
Whether it is treaty, ruin, warning, shrine, weapon, or superstition remains unknown.

## Aurelith / Uncanny Connection
Contact level: **2**.

0 — no Aurelith presence. 1 — rumor, taboo, religious reference, distant history. 2 — visible boundary, ruin, road, light, or artifact no one understands. 3 — direct interaction with Aurelith-made object. 4 — direct Aurelith appearance.

## Protagonist’s Feeling
Nera distrusts every explanation and notices how useful the fear is to men.

## What Changes Here
Starts as distant taboo; becomes the stage where human lies cannot hide behind banners.

## Visual Motif
white stones under rain

## Continuity Notes
Keep this location practical and plot-shaping. Do not add decorative lore that does not change a choice.
