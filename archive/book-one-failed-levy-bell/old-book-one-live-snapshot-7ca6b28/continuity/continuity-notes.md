## Current live direction

- Working title: **The Field Where No Banners Go**.
- The prior **The Bell on Starvel Hill** version is archived.
- Central conflict: expose a false human war levy before Pell is taken.
- Aurelith layer: Starvel Field as distant boundary, taboo, white stones, and human fear.
- No Aurelith POV, no active Aurelith legal machinery, no mirror-pillar, no Name-Iron.

## Chapter 1 fixed facts

- Nera is scraping Pell’s name off the levy roll when the bell rings.
- The opening is inside the Split Loaf bakehouse, not a decorative market overview.
- Osa is frightened enough not to stop Nera.
- Joryn sees the scrape and warns Nera to hide Pell.
- Osric arrives with riders and Lord Venn’s bronze boar sign.
- Halven Marr’s handwriting is on the roll.
- Pell arrives with a dead man’s boot and a letter from the burned tithe barn.
- The letter’s visible line: “If I am found burned, look to Venn’s own.”

## Organization sync

## Risks

- Too many named villagers can dilute the cast; compress if they do not affect plot.
- Starvel Field can become decorative if it does not shape a human choice.
- Osric must remain understandable without being excused.
- Aurelith mystery should humble human power, not solve human injustice.
