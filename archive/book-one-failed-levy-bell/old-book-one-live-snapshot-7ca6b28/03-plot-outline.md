# 03 - Plot outline

## Act 1: the levy lie

Low Wicker wakes to a war levy. Nera is already trying to keep Pell's name off the roll. Osric Venn arrives with riders, claiming a burned tithe barn proves enemy raiders have crossed the border. Grain and boys are demanded by sunset.

Pell brings Nera a letter found in the ash. It was hidden by a dead carter and names Venn's own men as the burners.

Nera cannot simply accuse Osric. Low Wicker depends on his road access and grain mercy. The reeve wants quiet. Osa wants Pell hidden. Joryn warns Nera that a forged roll can hang her.

## Act 2: the road around the field

The watched road is blocked. Sister Auma points Nera toward an older chapel way that skirts Starvel Field, a place no army crosses and no map agrees on.

Nera, Pell, and later Bruk move through human danger: patrols, debt men, rain, hunger, villagers who might sell them, and Joryn's divided loyalty. The Aurelith boundary remains mostly a shape in the landscape. Its power is shown by human behavior: lowered banners, avoided stones, horses refusing the old edge.

They gather proof that the fire was human: burn patterns, a cart mark, a debt tally, and the dead man's letter.

## Act 3: witness under no banner

The human court route fails or is compromised. Osric moves to march the levy before dawn. Nera forces a public witness at the edge of Starvel Field, where local custom makes armed banners lower and oath-breaking politically dangerous.

The climax is human. Nera reads the letter aloud, presents Bruk's road evidence, and makes Joryn choose whether to repeat a lie under witness. Starvel Field does not judge. Its silence only strips away the banners men hide behind.

## Resolution

Pell is spared the immediate levy. Low Wicker keeps some grain. Osric's claim is damaged but not erased. The war continues elsewhere. Nera has become a carrier of dangerous truth.

Starvel Field remains unexplained.

## What this story is not

- not an elf-law case
- not a chosen-child story
- not a rebellion epic
- not a lesson in Aurelith technology
- not a story where elves solve human injustice
