# Osric Venn — Voice and Style

## Speech Pattern
quiet, exact, legalistic when useful, insulting through politeness.

## Vocabulary
right, road peace, levy, witness, exemption, lawful, practical.

## What They Avoid Saying
mustache-twirling villainy.

## Emotional Leakage
his contempt shows when poor people read too well.

## Dialogue Examples
- "Right first, then we can talk about brave."
- "If there is a safe way, show me the cost before you name it."
- "That sounds like a lord’s word for a poor person’s trouble."

## Do Not Write Them Like
mustache-twirling villainy.
