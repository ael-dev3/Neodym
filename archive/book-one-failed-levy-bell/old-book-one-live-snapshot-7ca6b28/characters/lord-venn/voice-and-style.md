# Lord Venn — Voice and Style

## Speech Pattern
not on page; if quoted, formal and predatory through intermediaries.

## Vocabulary
levy, road peace, defense, tithe, banner.

## What They Avoid Saying
direct villain monologue.

## Emotional Leakage
power appears as paperwork and riders, not intimacy.

## Dialogue Examples
- "Levy first, then we can talk about brave."
- "If there is a safe way, show me the cost before you name it."
- "That sounds like a lord’s word for a poor person’s trouble."

## Do Not Write Them Like
direct villain monologue.
