# Nera Brindle — Visual Description

## Core Visual Identity
narrow, quick-eyed, rain-dark hair usually pinned badly, ink stains at the thumb, flour caught in cuffs when she forgets to wash before reading The visual should feel grounded in mud, wool, road, bread, ash, and human pressure.

## Face
Use specific rural human detail. Avoid perfect fantasy-model symmetry.

## Body / Movement
useful, dry, clever enough to be dangerous and poor enough to be ignored should shape how they stand, move, and handle tools.

## Clothing
plain wool dress, patched apron, knife sheath repurposed for a bread knife and scraping tool, cheap leather shoes that take water at the seam.

## Hands / Tools / Objects
scraped ink, bread knife, wet parchment.

## Color Palette
mud brown, rain gray, barley flour white, ink black.

## Symbolic Motif
a bread knife used to alter a roll.

## Image Generation Prompt
Grounded high-fantasy character portrait of Nera Brindle, low-born letter-reader, toll scribe, baker’s assistant, and protagonist, in rural Low Wicker, tactile wool, mud, bread ash, rain, iron, subdued natural light, emotionally restrained, human-first, no modern clothing, no glossy fantasy armor, no visible Aurelith explanation, painterly realism.

## Negative Prompt
No modern slang visuals, no sci-fi tech, no neon glow, no generic elf styling, no heroic chosen-one pose, no franchise imitation, no clean cosplay fabric.
