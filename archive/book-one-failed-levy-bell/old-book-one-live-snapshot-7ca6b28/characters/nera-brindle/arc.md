# Nera Brindle — Arc

## Starting Point
Believes quiet correction is safer than open accusation.

## Pressure Applied
Pell’s name on the roll and the dead carter’s letter make silence lethal.

## Key Turning Points
- First appearance: Chapter 1 - Levy Bell.
- Central pressure: she is the only person with enough literacy, courage, and love for Pell to move the proof.
- Planned chapter relevance: 1-12 planned; drafted in 1.

## Choice Under Pressure
Reads and carries dangerous proof in public despite class risk.

## Ending State
A road scribe or witness runner carrying hard truth beyond Low Wicker.

## Unresolved Tension
Whether truth-carrying becomes vocation, exile, or both.
