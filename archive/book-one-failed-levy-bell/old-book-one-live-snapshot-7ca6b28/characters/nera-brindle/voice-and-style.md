# Nera Brindle — Voice and Style

## Speech Pattern
short, dry, practical; she jokes when cornered but rarely wastes a word.

## Vocabulary
roll, witness, bread, knife, count, seal, road, enough.

## What They Avoid Saying
open declarations of bravery or modern political language.

## Emotional Leakage
her hands keep working when fear rises; jokes go sharper.

## Dialogue Examples
- "Roll first, then we can talk about brave."
- "If there is a safe way, show me the cost before you name it."
- "That sounds like a lord’s word for a poor person’s trouble."

## Do Not Write Them Like
open declarations of bravery or modern political language.
