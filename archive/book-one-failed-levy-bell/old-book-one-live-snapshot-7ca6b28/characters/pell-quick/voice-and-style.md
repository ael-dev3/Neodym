# Pell Quick — Voice and Style

## Speech Pattern
fast, defensive humor, asks blunt questions, dodges fear with appetite.

## Vocabulary
bell, bread, boot, I saw, not fair, quick, just asking.

## What They Avoid Saying
precocious prophecy wisdom or mystical insight.

## Emotional Leakage
the joke fails when he is truly scared.

## Dialogue Examples
- "Bell first, then we can talk about brave."
- "If there is a safe way, show me the cost before you name it."
- "That sounds like a lord’s word for a poor person’s trouble."

## Do Not Write Them Like
precocious prophecy wisdom or mystical insight.
