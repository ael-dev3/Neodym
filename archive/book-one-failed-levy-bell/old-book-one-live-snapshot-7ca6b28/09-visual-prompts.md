# 09 - Book One visual prompts

## Current direction

Book One is now **The Field Where No Banners Go**: a human-first border novella about a false war levy, a boy on the roll, a burned tithe barn, and the road around Starvel Field.

The Aurelith layer is thin. Use white stones, lowered banners, bad maps, and distant blue-white light only sparingly. Do not depict Aurelith officials, village-level elf law, Name-Irons, mirror-pillars, or silver-law machinery for the live Book One direction.

## Chapter 1 - Levy Bell

Prompt: Rainy bakehouse yard in a damp sheep village, low-born woman holding a scraped levy roll, ash-smeared orphan boy clutching a dead man's boot, stern human bailiff's man with wet riders behind him, lowered bronze-boar banner, villagers watching in fear, grounded high fantasy, mud, bread, rain, ash, wool.

Negative prompt: magical identity mark, glowing elf law, mirror pillar, direct elf presence, sci-fi technology, clean heroic fantasy poster.

## Low Wicker

Prompt: Rural border sheep valley in rain, thatched roofs, bakehouse smoke, small chapel, toll bridge, muddy road, villagers with carts and wool bundles, human life and human pressure first.

## Starvel Field

Prompt: Pale upland field above a village, white boundary stones in wet grass, road bending around it, no banners raised near the stones, horses uneasy, faint blue-white light far beyond the rain, mysterious and unexplained.

## The dead carter's letter

Prompt: Bloodstained folded letter hidden in the cut lining of a burned leather boot, ash and rain on the page, candlelit bakehouse table, human evidence rather than magic.

## Public witness scene

Prompt: Villagers and human riders gathered at the edge of a forbidden field under lowered banners, a woman reading a dead man's letter aloud, white stones behind them, tension from human law and fear, no supernatural verdict.
