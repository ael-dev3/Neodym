# Major Characters

## Mara Veyl

- **Race:** human.
- **Role:** funeral singer, bakery worker, emerging Emberwake symbol.
- **Age impression:** late twenties.
- **Appearance:** dark curls usually pinned under a flour scarf, warm brown skin, strong hands, small throat scar from an illegal childhood song lesson, tired eyes that brighten when she sings.
- **Personality:** practical, tender, sharp-tongued, slow to trust, quick to defend children and the dead.
- **Motivation:** recover her brother's stolen memory and stop families from being quietly erased.
- **Fear:** becoming a rebel who can justify any cruelty.
- **Secret:** her Bloodsong can carry memory without Neodym.
- **Moral contradiction:** she condemns collaborators but depends on one to save her cousin.
- **Relationships:** sister of dead Tovin; uneasy ally of Seraphel; protected and challenged by Sister Rhun; tempted to kill Cadran.
- **Relationship to Neodym:** distrusts it, yet must use stolen memory crystals.
- **Arc:** private mourner to public truth-singer.
- **Death/transformation risk:** voice could burn out; she could become a martyr-icon others misuse.
- **Signature motif:** red thread around burnt grain.

## Seraphel of the Quiet Third

- **Race:** Aurelith.
- **Role:** dissident prophecy engineer.
- **Age impression:** ageless face like a solemn thirty-year-old.
- **Appearance:** moon-pale skin with faint silver veins, black hair braided with broken Neodym thread, gray mirrored eyes, immaculate posture disrupted by bare unadorned hands.
- **Personality:** patient, ashamed, precise, unexpectedly gentle, capable of terrifying calm.
- **Motivation:** prevent the Concord from using failed prophecy to justify mass control.
- **Fear:** that his reforms are merely another form of custodianship.
- **Secret:** falsified a risk record to save children marked Red.
- **Moral contradiction:** opposes oppression while still instinctively thinking in permissions.
- **Relationships:** ally and foil to Mara; former student of Vaelis Thren; distrusted by Tham; pitied by Eloa.
- **Relationship to Neodym:** dependent on it for memory continuity, increasingly willing to sever that dependence.
- **Arc:** guilt-bound reformer to traitor for mercy.
- **Death/transformation risk:** Drift, execution, or loss of centuries of memory.
- **Signature motif:** cracked calculation ring.

## Lord Cadran Vale

- **Race:** human.
- **Role:** collaborator governor of Selachor.
- **Age impression:** mid-forties.
- **Appearance:** handsome, carefully groomed, olive skin, silver throat seal polished like jewelry, velvet coats in human colors cut to Aurelith symmetry.
- **Personality:** intelligent, diplomatic, exhausted, charming under pressure, morally evasive.
- **Motivation:** keep Selachor fed and avoid open slaughter.
- **Fear:** losing status and discovering he never had real power.
- **Secret:** maintains a hidden ledger of children he has saved and children he traded away.
- **Moral contradiction:** genuinely saves lives through collaboration while feeding the system that endangers them.
- **Relationships:** patron to Sealed schools; antagonist and possible reluctant ally to Mara; watched by Vaelis.
- **Relationship to Neodym:** wears refined seals as status and leash.
- **Arc:** compromise to confession, or compromise to damnation.
- **Death/transformation risk:** public execution by either side, or becoming a puppet emptied by memory correction.
- **Signature motif:** warm gold signet trapped behind silver seal.

## Sister Rhun

- **Race:** human.
- **Role:** underground prophet and Emberwake organizer.
- **Age impression:** elderly, but fierce.
- **Appearance:** shaved head, ash-marked forehead, red candle wax on sleeves, one milky eye, smile like a knife in bread.
- **Personality:** charismatic, loving, ruthless, funny, dangerous.
- **Motivation:** preserve the Laughing Flame and force history open.
- **Fear:** that moderation will bury another generation.
- **Secret:** once trained as a Still Cantor and knows Aurelith hymns.
- **Moral contradiction:** protects children yet considers civilian collaborators acceptable sacrifices.
- **Relationships:** mentor to Mara; enemy of Cadran; respects Kesh's oaths.
- **Relationship to Neodym:** calls it a shackle but uses memory crystals when needed.
- **Arc:** zeal toward costly restraint, or descent into holy violence.
- **Death/transformation risk:** martyrdom that radicalizes the Emberwake.
- **Signature motif:** cracked ash bowl with living ember.

## Vaelis Thren

- **Race:** Aurelith.
- **Role:** high prophecy engineer, antagonist, member of the Orrery Hands.
- **Age impression:** beautiful elder without visible age.
- **Appearance:** silver-white hair falling straight to the waist, black eyes with star flecks, robes layered like moonlit blades, hands stained faint blue from Neodym dust.
- **Personality:** courteous, brilliant, lonely, terrifyingly sincere.
- **Motivation:** prevent a second Sky Fever at any cost.
- **Fear:** that human freedom is necessary and that he has wasted centuries opposing it.
- **Secret:** knows the Wrong Prophecy is one of many failures.
- **Moral contradiction:** loves humanity abstractly while sacrificing actual humans.
- **Relationships:** former mentor to Seraphel; manipulates Cadran; fascinated by Mara.
- **Relationship to Neodym:** almost priestly dependence; may be more archive than person.
- **Arc:** hardliner to tragic self-knowledge, or architect of the Ninth Mercy.
- **Death/transformation risk:** merges with an Orrery to preserve control.
- **Signature motif:** black orrery halo behind the head.

## Tham Orewake

- **Race:** Cairnkin.
- **Role:** miner, brightstone sufferer, witness to buried history.
- **Age impression:** sturdy middle age.
- **Appearance:** basalt-gray skin, amber eyes, lichen tattoos at neck and knuckles, respirator veil, one shoulder enlarged by a glowing brightstone growth.
- **Personality:** dry humor, stubborn, loyal to workers before flags, suspicious of grand speeches.
- **Motivation:** get miners out alive and make the owners answer.
- **Fear:** his children inheriting poisoned tunnels.
- **Secret:** his brightstone visions show pre-Aurelith hands refining Neodym.
- **Moral contradiction:** sells ore illegally to feed families, strengthening the trade he hates.
- **Relationships:** distrusts Seraphel; respects Mara's grief; bonded to Cairnkin crews.
- **Relationship to Neodym:** poisoned by it and able to read it.
- **Arc:** local labor leader to keeper of world-changing evidence.
- **Death/transformation risk:** brightstone growth consumes his body into a living archive.
- **Signature motif:** cracked mining lamp glowing blue-silver.

## Iri of Black Salt

- **Race:** Nacren.
- **Role:** diplomat, smuggler, sea-court negotiator.
- **Age impression:** early thirties.
- **Appearance:** pearl-dark skin, bioluminescent blue scars along cheeks and wrists, finlike ear frills, black kelp-silk coat, shell mask worn at the belt.
- **Personality:** poised, amused, unsentimental, secretly compassionate.
- **Motivation:** secure Nacren sovereignty and protect sea routes from Concord expansion.
- **Fear:** land wars dragging the sea courts into ruin.
- **Secret:** their reef city is suffering from Neodym contamination.
- **Moral contradiction:** sells passage to refugees while charging prices that exclude the poorest.
- **Relationships:** bargains with Mara; flirts with danger around Seraphel; owes a debt to Caltrow brokers.
- **Relationship to Neodym:** studies how saltwater changes it.
- **Arc:** neutral profiteer to committed ally.
- **Death/transformation risk:** exile from tide courts or drowning in a moon-fragment vision.
- **Signature motif:** black salt poured over a silver seal.

## Kesh Gravetongue

- **Race:** Vharakul.
- **Role:** auxiliary captain and reluctant protector.
- **Age impression:** seasoned warrior.
- **Appearance:** tall, broad, horned brow, tawny storm-patterned hide, braided mane, red oath scars across chest and jaw.
- **Personality:** blunt, observant, disciplined, unexpectedly poetic.
- **Motivation:** keep his clan safe under treaty.
- **Fear:** oath-breaking that leaves his children unprotected.
- **Secret:** human rebels saved his daughter from plague when the Concord delayed medicine.
- **Moral contradiction:** guards the system that once failed him.
- **Relationships:** respects Sister Rhun; ordered to capture Mara; uneasy subordinate to Aurelith officers.
- **Relationship to Neodym:** distrusts it; wears only treaty tokens, never body seals.
- **Arc:** lawful enforcer to oath-breaker in defense of life.
- **Death/transformation risk:** execution by treaty law or exile from clan.
- **Signature motif:** glaive wrapped with a child's bead cord.

## Eloa of the Blank Kilns

- **Race:** Eirrem.
- **Role:** ghost witness and failed immortality survivor.
- **Age impression:** shifts between child, adult, and elder depending on memory.
- **Appearance:** translucent ash-white skin, black veil floating as if underwater, wax seal fused over the heart, face difficult to remember after looking away.
- **Personality:** mournful, sly, fragmented, startlingly kind.
- **Motivation:** recover their name and testify before dissolving.
- **Fear:** being stored in a Moon Vault forever.
- **Secret:** remembers the Unlit Seat speaking with a human voice.
- **Moral contradiction:** steals memories from the living to stabilize their own identity.
- **Relationships:** drawn to Seraphel's guilt; frightens Mara; hunted by Moon Archivists.
- **Relationship to Neodym:** Neodym both preserves and chains them.
- **Arc:** haunting to chosen testimony.
- **Death/transformation risk:** final dissolution or forced archival binding.
- **Signature motif:** white ash handprint on black glass.

## Nym Sable

- **Race:** human child altered by Neodym exposure.
- **Role:** marked Red liminal figure.
- **Age impression:** eleven.
- **Appearance:** slight, quick, one brown eye and one silver-flecked eye, ordinary patched clothes, seal burn on wrist, pockets full of wire birds.
- **Personality:** curious, frightened, stubborn, funny at the wrong times.
- **Motivation:** stay with family and avoid becoming anyone's prophecy.
- **Fear:** being taken to a Lunary.
- **Secret:** hears voices from raw Neodym before it is refined.
- **Moral contradiction:** can expose lies but sometimes lies to keep adults from fighting.
- **Relationships:** protected by Mara; sought by Seraphel and Vaelis for different reasons; befriended by Pip.
- **Relationship to Neodym:** living receiver of mineral memory.
- **Arc:** object of fear to chooser of impossible future.
- **Death/transformation risk:** Clarification, Drift-like overload, or voluntary transformation.
- **Signature motif:** tiny bread-crust bird with silver wire wings.

## Pip-of-Nine-Bells

- **Race:** Caltrow Kith.
- **Role:** broker, message-breaker, memory smuggler.
- **Age impression:** adult but theatrical.
- **Appearance:** small foxlike face, bright black eyes, long fingers, rust-red patchwork coat, nine cracked bells sewn where buttons should be.
- **Personality:** comic, evasive, greedy, soft-hearted in ways they deny.
- **Motivation:** stay unowned and always know the price of a secret.
- **Fear:** becoming predictable.
- **Secret:** once made an archive forget a child and cannot restore the name.
- **Moral contradiction:** saves people by selling pieces of them.
- **Relationships:** bargains with Mara; owes Iri; annoys Seraphel; protects Nym.
- **Relationship to Neodym:** makes it misbehave by proximity.
- **Arc:** self-serving trickster to responsible misrule.
- **Death/transformation risk:** sealed into a pattern cage by Mirror Veil agents.
- **Signature motif:** cracked bell that rings one minute before danger.
