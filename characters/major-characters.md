# Major characters

## Book One cast

See `book-one/02-characters.md` for the active Book One cast.

- Nera Brindle: letter-reader and toll scribe.
- Pell Quick: orphaned errand boy on the levy roll.
- Osa Fen: baker and innkeeper.
- Osric Venn: local debt-holder and bailiff's man.
- Joryn Hale: local guard with a bad oath.
- Sister Auma: chapel keeper.
- Bruk of the Low Cairns: stonemender and road guide.

## Larger-world character slots

### Human king who fears the sea

A king who treats the Aurelith like a natural force: not a peer, not a master, but something whose silence can ruin a campaign.

### Border priest

A priest whose theology cannot decide whether elder folk are angels, devils, or irrelevant neighbors.

### Noble relic heir

A young noble inherits an Aurelith object that confers prestige but no understanding.

### Market league widow

A trader who knows more about road politics than knights do and uses old taboos as commercial weapons.

## Rule

Do not make the Aurelith the center of every character's life. Most people should want food, safety, revenge, status, love, land, forgiveness, or a road through winter.
