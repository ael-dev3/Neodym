# Character Archetypes

## 1. The Emberwake Singer

- **Race:** human.
- **Age impression:** late twenties.
- **Social status:** working-class funeral singer.
- **Motivation:** recover stolen grief and make the dead matter.
- **Flaw:** anger sharpens into cruelty when collaborators are near.
- **Secret:** carries Bloodsong strong enough to crack mirrors.
- **Possible arc:** private mourner to public revolutionary who refuses revenge.
- **Central conflict relationship:** proves mortal emotion can defeat predictive control.
- **Visual identity:** worn red scarf, flour dust, throat scar, candle-lit eyes.
- **Symbolic object:** burnt grain stalk wrapped in red thread.
- **Possible first scene:** singing at a funeral and hearing the missing echo.

## 2. The Quiet Dissident

- **Race:** Aurelith.
- **Age impression:** ageless, early thirties in face.
- **Social status:** minor Orrery noble.
- **Motivation:** repair the harm of his own civilization without causing collapse.
- **Flaw:** apologizes instead of acting until forced.
- **Secret:** falsified one prophecy to save human children.
- **Arc:** guilt to risky solidarity.
- **Relationship:** bridge between oppressor and rebellion.
- **Visual identity:** tarnished silver robes, cracked mirror pin, bare hands.
- **Object:** a Neodym calculation ring with one missing segment.
- **First scene:** kneeling in flour dust in a human bakery.

## 3. The Sealed Collaborator

- **Race:** human.
- **Age impression:** forties.
- **Social status:** provincial governor.
- **Motivation:** prevent massacres through compromise.
- **Flaw:** mistakes access for power.
- **Secret:** protects some marked children while condemning others.
- **Arc:** cowardly moderation to costly truth or tragic betrayal.
- **Relationship:** shows why collaboration is morally tangled.
- **Visual identity:** elegant human clothing constrained by silver throat seal.
- **Object:** warm signet ring over cold Name-Iron.
- **First scene:** offering condolences while hiding a warrant.

## 4. The Memory Smuggler

- **Race:** Caltrow Kith or human.
- **Age impression:** unknowable adult.
- **Social status:** black-market broker.
- **Motivation:** profit, survival, and occasional impossible kindness.
- **Flaw:** treats other people's grief as currency.
- **Secret:** once sold a memory that led to a massacre.
- **Arc:** trickster to accountable ally.
- **Relationship:** moves forbidden truth through cracks in the system.
- **Visual identity:** patchwork coat, cracked bells, wax teeth, bright eyes.
- **Object:** a hollow tooth containing a childhood memory.
- **First scene:** bargaining under a bridge as patrols pass overhead.

## 5. The Candle Prophet

- **Race:** human.
- **Age impression:** elderly or prematurely aged.
- **Social status:** underground priest.
- **Motivation:** keep forbidden creation stories alive.
- **Flaw:** welcomes martyrdom too eagerly.
- **Secret:** once served as a Still Cantor.
- **Arc:** zeal to mercy.
- **Relationship:** spiritual counterweight to Aurelith order.
- **Visual identity:** ash-stained fingers, red candle crown, patched black robe.
- **Object:** cracked bowl of hearth ash.
- **First scene:** teaching children a myth that changes each telling.

## 6. The Border Soldier

- **Race:** Vharakul.
- **Age impression:** mature warrior.
- **Social status:** auxiliary captain.
- **Motivation:** protect clan hostages and border villages.
- **Flaw:** obeys cruel orders too long.
- **Secret:** owes a life-debt to human rebels.
- **Arc:** treaty-bound soldier to oath-breaker for justice.
- **Relationship:** embodies loyalty under coercion.
- **Visual identity:** horned silhouette, oath scars, storm-hide cloak.
- **Object:** curved glaive with adopted child's bead tied to haft.
- **First scene:** stopping a riot without drawing blood.

## 7. The Archive Scholar

- **Race:** Aurelith or human collaborator.
- **Age impression:** ageless or young adult.
- **Social status:** Moon Vault apprentice.
- **Motivation:** preserve truth.
- **Flaw:** confuses preservation with consent.
- **Secret:** has read a sealed contradiction in the Sky Fever records.
- **Arc:** curator to whistleblower.
- **Relationship:** reveals memory as sacred and political.
- **Visual identity:** inkless silver stylus, pale gloves, sleepless eyes.
- **Object:** blank archive key that opens forbidden shelves.
- **First scene:** cataloguing a memory that cries.

## 8. The Dream Thief

- **Race:** Nacren or human.
- **Age impression:** young adult.
- **Social status:** black-market specialist.
- **Motivation:** steal nightmares before they become evidence.
- **Flaw:** addicted to other lives.
- **Secret:** cannot dream naturally anymore.
- **Arc:** parasite to protector.
- **Relationship:** demonstrates privacy as resistance.
- **Visual identity:** salt mask, shell needles, dark wet cloak.
- **Object:** pearl vial of stolen nightmare.
- **First scene:** removing a child's dream under a mirror well.

## 9. The Logic-Priest

- **Race:** Aurelith.
- **Age impression:** serene elder.
- **Social status:** Still Cantor.
- **Motivation:** prevent harm through sacred order.
- **Flaw:** compassion filtered through doctrine becomes cruelty.
- **Secret:** envies human prayer.
- **Arc:** servant of law to witness against law.
- **Relationship:** gives the regime a sincere face.
- **Visual identity:** silver mouth veil, geometric robes, prayer abacus.
- **Object:** hymn blade used to cut false testimony.
- **First scene:** calming a crowd before arrests begin.

## 10. The Exile of the Blank Kilns

- **Race:** Eirrem.
- **Age impression:** shifting.
- **Social status:** legally ambiguous ghost-person.
- **Motivation:** recover a name or achieve final death.
- **Flaw:** manipulates the living through pity.
- **Secret:** remembers the face of the Unlit Seat.
- **Arc:** haunting to testimony.
- **Relationship:** living evidence of hidden atrocity.
- **Visual identity:** ash-white skin, blurred edges, black veil.
- **Object:** wax seal fused to the sternum.
- **First scene:** appearing in a room everyone later forgets.

## 11. The Other-Race Diplomat

- **Race:** Nacren, Thallor, or Cairnkin.
- **Age impression:** adult.
- **Social status:** envoy.
- **Motivation:** secure sovereignty for their people.
- **Flaw:** treats human suffering as leverage.
- **Secret:** their homeland is already compromised.
- **Arc:** neutrality to chosen risk.
- **Relationship:** widens the conflict beyond humans and Aurelith.
- **Visual identity:** pearl diplomatic mask, tide scars, layered treaty cords.
- **Object:** shell map that changes with moonlight.
- **First scene:** offering help with terms no desperate rebel wants to accept.

## 12. The Liminal Child

- **Race:** human altered by Neodym, or rare human-Aurelith lineage.
- **Age impression:** child or adolescent.
- **Social status:** marked Red.
- **Motivation:** be loved without being used.
- **Flaw:** fear makes their power lash out.
- **Secret:** can hear Neodym memories without a mirror.
- **Arc:** object of prophecy to chooser of future.
- **Relationship:** proof that categories are failing.
- **Visual identity:** one silver-flecked eye, ordinary patched clothes, bright seal burn.
- **Object:** toy bird made of bread crust and wire.
- **First scene:** failing Measure Day by singing a song no one taught them.
