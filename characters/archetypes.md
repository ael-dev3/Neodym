# Character archetypes

## Human-first archetypes

### The letter-reader

Low status, literate, useful to everyone, protected by no one. Reads secrets for people who outrank her and learns which words are dangerous.

### The levy child

A child made adult by a list. Not chosen, not magical, not noble. The story cares because someone loves him.

### The practical guardian

Keeps people alive through caution, hiding, food, and compromise. Can look cowardly until the bill comes due.

### The bad-oath guard

A decent person inside an indecent order. Useful because he shows how ordinary enforcement works.

### The debt-holder

Not a dark lord. A local human who turns hunger, records, and war into power.

### The taboo-edge guide

Someone outside ordinary trust who knows roads, stones, weather, and old boundaries better than officials do.

## Aurelith-related archetypes for later stories

### The false-favor noble

Claims ancient Aurelith contact to justify rank. The claim may be false, misunderstood, or irrelevant.

### The envoy witness

A human who once saw an Aurelith envoy and has spent years being disbelieved, exploited, or feared.

### The relic keeper

Owns something Aurelith-made and cannot use it, sell it, or safely admit ignorance.
