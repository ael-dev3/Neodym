# Novel outline

## Purpose

This file sketches possible longer-form stories after Book One. It is not active Book One canon.

The thin-veil rule still applies: the human world comes first. Aurelith presence should shape borders, myths, relics, diplomacy, and fear without turning every plot into elf administration.

## Possible Novel One direction: The Roadless Treaty

### Premise

A minor human kingdom prepares a border campaign after a dynastic murder. The fastest invasion route crosses a roadless territory marked by old white towers that no army has entered in three centuries. The king's council argues whether the taboo is holy law, cowardice, treaty, or superstition. A low-ranking human clerk discovers that several noble houses are lying about the treaty to hide a succession crime.

### Human engine

- succession murder
- rival noble claims
- grain routes and winter supply
- mercenary contracts
- church pressure
- border villages used as bargaining pieces
- a forged treaty translation

### Aurelith layer

- silent towers on the invasion route
- a diplomatic absence humans interpret too eagerly
- a relic seal in a noble vault that nobody understands
- old maps that refuse to agree about the roadless territory

No Aurelith explains the truth. The climax should be a human political choice made under the shadow of something older.

## Possible Novel Two direction: The Saint-Metal Vault

A market league inherits an Aurelith-made object it cannot use, sell, melt, or understand. Rival human factions turn the relic into proof of legitimacy. The plot is a city crime and inheritance story first, with the relic functioning as dangerous prestige rather than a solved machine.

## Possible Novel Three direction: The Bannerless Winter

A winter campaign collapses because no army will cross a forbidden field. Refugees, deserters, priests, and hungry townspeople suffer for a taboo whose origin no one agrees on. The story follows human consequences, not Aurelith motives.

## Saga bridge

Later novels may increase contact level gradually:

- Book One/early novellas: Levels 0-2.
- First major novel: mostly Levels 1-3.
- Mid-saga: rare Level 4 diplomatic contact.
- Late saga only: Level 5 direct intervention, used as disaster or miracle, never as exposition lecture.
