# Novella Outline — *The Song the Mirrors Missed*

## Format target

Approx. 30,000 words. A tight entry story focused on one city, one mine disaster, one stolen memory, and one impossible alliance.

## Core pitch

A human funeral singer discovers that her dead brother's memory was stolen before burial. To recover it, she must work with an Aurelith dissident whose own prophecy engine has begun to fail. Together they uncover evidence that a coming rebellion and a planned crackdown are both being manipulated by a hidden office of the Quiet Concord.

## Main character

**Mara Veyl**, human Bloodsong singer, public role as a bakery assistant and funeral vocalist in Selachor. She is warm, angry, practical, and terrified that rebellion will turn her into the kind of person her brother warned against becoming.

## Required story elements

- **Inciting incident:** Mara sings at her brother Tovin's funeral and realizes the grief does not echo correctly; his final memory has been removed.
- **Central conflict:** recover the stolen memory before the Mirror Choir uses it to justify mass arrests.
- **Major twist:** the memory proves Tovin was carrying a prophecy error, not a rebel plan. The Concord wants to hide that its predictions are failing.
- **Ending direction:** Mara releases the memory publicly through a forbidden song, preventing one massacre but making herself the first face of a larger uprising.
- **World revealed:** Selachor, Name-Irons, bread ledgers, Measure Day, Aurelith reformers, memory smuggling, Bloodsong, the Mirror Choir.
- **Good entry point because:** it keeps the world intimate while revealing the larger moral conflict through one family, one city, and one compromised dissident.

## Main locations

- Selachor bakery district.
- Public census square.
- Moonlit mirror court.
- Redroot Warrens under the old aqueduct.
- Glassmeadow collaborator school.
- A small Moon Vault annex.
- The Star Road outside the city.

## Main factions

- Emberwake cell led by Sister Rhun.
- Sealed House Vale.
- Mirror Veil agents.
- Mercy Heresy dissidents.
- Unsealed Hand memory smugglers.
- Province Watch.

## Emotional arc

Mara begins wanting private justice for her brother. She becomes tempted by revenge against every collaborator. By the end she chooses public truth over blood revenge, understanding that liberation must protect even frightened loyalists if it wants to be different from the Concord.

## Moral dilemma

If Mara releases the stolen memory, she may save rebels from false charges but expose a prophecy failure that triggers panic and harsher rule. If she hides it, her brother's death becomes another quiet correction.

## Chapter beats

### 1. Funeral Bread

Mara sings over Tovin's body in a crowded bakery chapel. The song should carry his last feeling back to the family, but the echo is hollow. A Mirror Cantor watching the rite marks Mara's grief as unstable.

### 2. The Name-Iron Bruise

Mara's seal burns with a summons. At the mirror court she sees Tovin's official death record: obedient, accidental, complete. She knows the record is too clean. Lord Cadran Vale offers sympathy and warns her not to dig.

### 3. Wrong Note Under the Bridge

A Caltrow broker named Pip-of-Nine-Bells sells Mara a clue: Tovin's memory passed through a sealed bread ledger. Payment is not coin but one happy memory of Tovin, which Mara refuses to surrender.

### 4. The Dissident in Silver

Seraphel, an Aurelith reformer, approaches Mara in a human district wearing a stained cloak to avoid notice. He admits an Orrery prediction failed the same night Tovin died. Mara nearly exposes him to a crowd.

### 5. Measure Day

Mara's young cousin Lio is tested and marked Red. The family must choose between compliance, flight, or bribery. This forces Mara into the Emberwake's Redroot Warrens.

### 6. The Redroot Warrens

Sister Rhun offers help but demands Tovin's stolen memory if recovered. Mara sees rebel courage and rebel cruelty: a collaborator prisoner is beaten for information.

### 7. Moon Vault Annex

Mara, Seraphel, and Tham Orewake enter a low archive under Selachor using a Cairnkin service tunnel. They find thousands of grief-tithe memories sorted like bottled rain.

### 8. The Brother's Last Sight

They recover Tovin's memory. He witnessed the Mirror Choir predict a rebel massacre that never happened, then saw agents planning to create evidence after the fact.

### 9. Bread Riot Mercy

The Province Watch moves early. Food ledgers close. A crowd forms. Vharakul soldiers hesitate as human children cry. Mara can turn the crowd into a riot with one song.

### 10. The Collaborator's Choice

Lord Cadran Vale reveals he protected some children marked Red by falsifying records, but also named Tovin to save his own house. He offers Mara safe passage if she stays silent.

### 11. The Song the Mirrors Missed

In census square, Mara sings Tovin's stolen memory inside a funeral melody altered by Bloodsong. Mirrors crack because they can record sound but not the mortal choice embedded in it.

### 12. First Ember

Mass arrests fail, but the city does not become free. Seraphel is exposed as a dissident. Cadran is ruined or forced deeper into compromise. Mara escapes through the bread tunnels carrying Lio, now a symbol she never asked to become.

## Visual set pieces

- Funeral in a bakery chapel, bread cooling beside a body.
- A child under silver measuring rods as family members hold their breath.
- Moon Vault bottles glowing with stolen grief.
- Census square mirrors cracking during a public song.
- An Aurelith dissident kneeling in flour dust to beg a human for trust.

## Open development questions

- Does Cadran die, become antagonist, or become a compromised ally?
- Is Tovin's memory complete or edited before Mara finds it?
- What exactly makes Lio marked Red?
- Should the ending reveal the Unlit Seat directly or only imply it?
