# Novella Outline — *The Bell on Starvel Hill*

The active first-book / first-novella outline now lives in [`book-one/`](../book-one/).

## Format target

A short, intimate human-perspective high-fantasy novella designed for **80–100 pages**.

Planning target: **20,000–25,000 words**.

Hard cap: **100 pages / 25,000 words** unless the repo owner explicitly changes it.

## Core pitch

In the rural sheep valley of Low Wicker, a low-born village letter-reader must save an orphaned bell-runner from being taken under unreadable silver law after an old bridge song wakes a distant Aurelith beacon on forbidden Starvel Hill.

## Core scale rule

This is not the grand epic version of Neodym. This is not the full rebellion story. This is not an explanation of Aurelith society.

The story remains:

- Human POV only.
- Rural and local.
- Medieval / high fantasy in material culture.
- Focused on one village, one road, one bridge, one chapel, one local court, one marked child, and one forbidden hill.
- Built around discrimination shown through practical limits rather than speeches.
- Restrained about Aurelith power, which appears through distant light, unreadable law, human collaborators, mirrors, marks, and road restrictions.

## Current ending direction

Pell is spared from immediate correction. The local bridge record is corrected. The Bridge-Bell Rhyme survives in hidden, altered form. Nera leaves Low Wicker with the full song disguised inside a flour account.

The valley remains under silver law. Starvel Hill remains unexplained. The larger world remains mostly intact and mostly unjust.
