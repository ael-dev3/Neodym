# Joryn Hale — Profile

## Role in Story
local guard whose poor man’s oath gives enforcement a familiar face.

## First Appearance
Chapter 1 - Levy Bell — `book-one/manuscript/chapter-01-levy-bell.md`.

## Later Appearances
1-2, 5, 7-8, 10-11 planned; drafted in 1.

## Age Impression
26.

## Social Class / Status
poor local man wearing a badge for richer men.

## Occupation
village guard / levy escort.

## Physical Description
wet shoulders, tired face, service-stiff posture, badge often sitting crooked.

## Clothing / Material Details
plain coat with local badge, spear, worn boots, cheap belt.

## Public Personality
decent enough to be trusted and obedient enough to be dangerous.

## Private Fear
that one brave choice will leave his mother hungry and him hanged for oath-breaking.

## Immediate Desire
keep order and keep Nera from forcing him to choose.

## Long-Term Desire
be a good man without losing the wage that feeds his house.

## Moral Flaw
waits for someone else to make courage unavoidable.

## Hidden Wound
poverty turned duty into a trap.

## Relationship to Central Conflict
he can help protect Pell or help deliver him.

## What They Know
orders, patrol timing, how Osric means to move Pell.

## What They Misunderstand
thinks delay is neutrality.

## What They Would Never Say Aloud
I know which side is wrong, and I still put on the badge..

## Key Object or Visual Motif
crooked badge, spear, looking away first.

## Continuity Notes
Keep this character’s function human, local, and causally tied to the levy-letter conflict. Do not expand them into unrelated lore.
