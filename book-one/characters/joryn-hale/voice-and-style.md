# Joryn Hale — Voice and Style

## Speech Pattern
plain, tense, warning-heavy; says names when frightened.

## Vocabulary
orders, roll, hide him, oath, Nera, don’t.

## What They Avoid Saying
heroic guard swagger.

## Emotional Leakage
looks away before lying.

## Dialogue Examples
- "Orders first, then we can talk about brave."
- "If there is a safe way, show me the cost before you name it."
- "That sounds like a lord’s word for a poor person’s trouble."

## Do Not Write Them Like
heroic guard swagger.
