# Sister Auma — Profile

## Role in Story
keeper of Saint Orra’s chapel and old witness customs.

## First Appearance
Chapter 3 - The Split Loaf Oath (planned) — `book-one/chapter-guides/chapter-03-the-split-loaf-oath.md`.

## Later Appearances
3-4, 8, 10, 12 planned.

## Age Impression
older adult.

## Social Class / Status
religious keeper with weak but real local authority.

## Occupation
chapel keeper, witness custodian.

## Physical Description
small, dry hands, candle-smoke in clothes, eyes that notice what is omitted.

## Clothing / Material Details
plain chapel wool, key-ring at belt, patched veil, wax-specked sleeves.

## Public Personality
quiet, unsettling, more practical than pious.

## Private Fear
that the old customs are too weak when finally needed.

## Immediate Desire
get the letter witnessed without making the chapel an easy target.

## Long-Term Desire
keep truth alive in forms power still has to respect.

## Moral Flaw
speaks in fragments because full answers cost too much.

## Hidden Wound
has watched records fail the poor before.

## Relationship to Central Conflict
offers Nera a route and moral frame but cannot carry the risk for her.

## What They Know
west-door oaths, chapel roads, which old customs still frighten nobles.

## What They Misunderstand
may overestimate what forms can protect when men are desperate.

## What They Would Never Say Aloud
The chapel has survived by letting some truths die outside its door..

## Key Object or Visual Motif
keys, wax, west door.

## Continuity Notes
Keep this character’s function human, local, and causally tied to the levy-letter conflict. Do not expand them into unrelated lore.
