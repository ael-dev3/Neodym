# Sister Auma — Voice and Style

## Speech Pattern
measured, elliptical, practical under holy phrasing.

## Vocabulary
witness, west door, oath, child, seal, enough, not here.

## What They Avoid Saying
mystic exposition dump.

## Emotional Leakage
answers questions with actions: keys, candles, hidden papers.

## Dialogue Examples
- "Witness first, then we can talk about brave."
- "If there is a safe way, show me the cost before you name it."
- "That sounds like a lord’s word for a poor person’s trouble."

## Do Not Write Them Like
mystic exposition dump.
