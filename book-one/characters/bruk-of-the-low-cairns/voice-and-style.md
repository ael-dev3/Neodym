# Bruk of the Low Cairns — Voice and Style

## Speech Pattern
plain, tactile, road-specific; distrusts fancy law words.

## Vocabulary
rut, stone, undercut, water, weight, wrong burn, see here.

## What They Avoid Saying
exotic outsider mysticism.

## Emotional Leakage
names physical facts when feelings get close.

## Dialogue Examples
- "Rut first, then we can talk about brave."
- "If there is a safe way, show me the cost before you name it."
- "That sounds like a lord’s word for a poor person’s trouble."

## Do Not Write Them Like
exotic outsider mysticism.
