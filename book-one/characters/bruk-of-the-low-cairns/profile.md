# Bruk of the Low Cairns — Profile

## Role in Story
Cairnkin stonemender and road guide who reads road evidence.

## First Appearance
Chapter 5 - The Hedge Knight's Price (planned) — `book-one/chapter-guides/chapter-05-the-hedge-knights-price.md`.

## Later Appearances
5-12 planned.

## Age Impression
adult.

## Social Class / Status
Cairnkin outsider trusted for labor and mistrusted for personhood.

## Occupation
stonemender, guide, road reader.

## Physical Description
stone-dust in creases, broad hands, deliberate gait, weathered face.

## Clothing / Material Details
patched road coat, leather tool roll, knee wrappings, boots repaired with mismatched thongs.

## Public Personality
useful, suspect, blunt when paid poorly.

## Private Fear
being used as proof and then blamed when proof becomes dangerous.

## Immediate Desire
be treated as witness, not just tool.

## Long-Term Desire
make road knowledge count as knowledge.

## Moral Flaw
keeps score of slights and can make allies work to earn trust.

## Hidden Wound
years of humans passing fear downward onto Cairnkin.

## Relationship to Central Conflict
his evidence helps expose the false fire and route.

## What They Know
roadbeds, burn marks, cart traces, shifted stones, boundary customs.

## What They Misunderstand
expects Nera to discard him when locals turn sour.

## What They Would Never Say Aloud
I am tired of fixing roads for people who spit when I walk them..

## Key Object or Visual Motif
stone hammer, cart ruts, low cairn dust.

## Continuity Notes
Keep this character’s function human, local, and causally tied to the levy-letter conflict. Do not expand them into unrelated lore.
