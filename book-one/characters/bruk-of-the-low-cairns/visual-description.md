# Bruk of the Low Cairns — Visual Description

## Core Visual Identity
stone-dust in creases, broad hands, deliberate gait, weathered face The visual should feel grounded in mud, wool, road, bread, ash, and human pressure.

## Face
Use specific rural human detail. Avoid perfect fantasy-model symmetry.

## Body / Movement
useful, suspect, blunt when paid poorly should shape how they stand, move, and handle tools.

## Clothing
patched road coat, leather tool roll, knee wrappings, boots repaired with mismatched thongs.

## Hands / Tools / Objects
stone hammer, cart ruts, low cairn dust.

## Color Palette
stone gray, peat black, moss green, worn leather.

## Symbolic Motif
road hammer and chalked stone.

## Image Generation Prompt
Grounded high-fantasy character portrait of Bruk of the Low Cairns, Cairnkin stonemender and road guide who reads road evidence, in rural Low Wicker, tactile wool, mud, bread ash, rain, iron, subdued natural light, emotionally restrained, human-first, no modern clothing, no glossy fantasy armor, no visible Aurelith explanation, painterly realism.

## Negative Prompt
No modern slang visuals, no sci-fi tech, no neon glow, no generic elf styling, no heroic chosen-one pose, no franchise imitation, no clean cosplay fabric.
