# Osa Fen — Profile

## Role in Story
owner of the Split Loaf, baker, widow, and Nera’s guardian figure.

## First Appearance
Chapter 1 - Levy Bell — `book-one/manuscript/chapter-01-levy-bell.md`.

## Later Appearances
1-5, 8, 11-12 planned; drafted in 1.

## Age Impression
late 40s or early 50s.

## Social Class / Status
widowed tradeswoman with a license and ovens to lose.

## Occupation
baker, innkeeper, informal shelter keeper.

## Physical Description
broad-shouldered, flour to the elbows, strong arms, face lined by oven heat and worry.

## Clothing / Material Details
rolled sleeves, heavy apron, dark skirt dusted white, kerchief that never stays clean.

## Public Personality
hard, practical, scolding, reliable when no one calls it charity.

## Private Fear
that one brave act can close her ovens and starve everyone she feeds.

## Immediate Desire
keep Pell hidden and keep the Split Loaf open.

## Long-Term Desire
protect her makeshift family without inviting ruin.

## Moral Flaw
turns fear into commandments and calls it wisdom.

## Hidden Wound
widowhood taught her how quickly law turns shelter into liability.

## Relationship to Central Conflict
her ovens are both refuge and hostage.

## What They Know
who owes bread, who can hide, who will inform for grain.

## What They Misunderstand
believes bending early enough can always prevent breaking.

## What They Would Never Say Aloud
I love these children enough to be stupid for them..

## Key Object or Visual Motif
oven peel, flour arms, blessing cuts in bread.

## Continuity Notes
Keep this character’s function human, local, and causally tied to the levy-letter conflict. Do not expand them into unrelated lore.
