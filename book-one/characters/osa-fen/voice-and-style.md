# Osa Fen — Voice and Style

## Speech Pattern
blunt, domestic, cutting; love hides inside scolding.

## Vocabulary
bread, oven, fool, license, flour, winter, close the door.

## What They Avoid Saying
soft inspirational speeches.

## Emotional Leakage
feeds people while telling them they are idiots.

## Dialogue Examples
- "Bread first, then we can talk about brave."
- "If there is a safe way, show me the cost before you name it."
- "That sounds like a lord’s word for a poor person’s trouble."

## Do Not Write Them Like
soft inspirational speeches.
