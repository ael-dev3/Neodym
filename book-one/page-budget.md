## Compression mode rules

When estimated manuscript exceeds 22,500 words or 90 pages:

- Stop adding new scenes unless explicitly instructed by the owner.
- Combine repeated clue scenes.
- Cut expository dialogue.
- Replace generic description with fewer, sharper local details.
- Move worldbuilding into implication.
- Tighten chapter hooks.
- Preserve the same premise, protagonist, setting, and ending unless owner feedback changes them.
