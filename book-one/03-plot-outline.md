# 03 - Plot outline

## Act 1: the false fair copy

Elsbet Vale is preparing the quarterly chapel fair copy when Ruan Kest arrives early with a court summons. The flood court has been moved up. The fair copy must be sealed by dusk.

Elsbet notices that Ruan's demanded wording omits Maura Dain's marriage. In the rough chapel register, Maura's marriage line exists. So does a ninth burial candle note tied to her married name.

If Elsbet writes the omission, Wren Dain can be declared kinless or base-born for property purposes, letting the manor lease Nine-Candle Meadow and its ferry landing to Ruan's cousin.

Elsbet cannot simply accuse Ruan. She has one rough entry, a weak priest, and a debt against her household. She needs corroboration before the fair copy is sealed.

## Act 2: proof with human costs

Elsbet seeks proof through plausible channels:

- Father Orlan's memory and seal authority;
- Matra Voss's candle receipt slats;
- Wren's marriage cord;
- ferry lease marks at Nine-Candle Meadow;
- court timing that reveals Ruan's motive.

Each step creates cost. Matra risks her candle contract. Wren risks faster wardship seizure. Father Orlan risks the chapel's seal right. Elsbet risks her brother's debt bond.

The Glass Mile remains at the edge: an old white-stone route no banner crosses. It shapes why the ferry matters during floodwater, but it does not intervene.

Ruan escalates by moving the hearing earlier and threatening to enter the manor copy without chapel correction.

## Act 3: the candle count

At the flood court, Elsbet cannot prove moral truth. She can force procedural doubt.

She presents the rough chapel entry, Matra's receipt slat, Wren's marriage cord, and the mismatch in Ruan's fair-copy wording. She demands that the burial candles be counted according to chapel custom before Maura's name is entered.

Ruan tries to reduce the contradiction to poor memory and clerical error. Harl Brey, the steward, does not become a hero. He delays the wardship and ferry transfer because a dirty entry could be appealed.

Wren is placed under temporary chapel ward until harvest hearing. Nine-Candle Meadow is not secured forever, but it is not stolen today.

## Resolution

Elsbet wins a narrow human victory:

- Maura's married name survives in the record.
- Wren is not sent to Ruan's cousin's kitchen immediately.
- The ferry transfer is delayed.
- Ruan is checked but not destroyed.
- Father Orlan's chapel keeps its seal for now, under scrutiny.
- Elsbet's household debt is still dangerous.

The Glass Mile remains unexplained.

## What this story is not

- not a chosen-one plot;
- not a direct Aurelith mystery;
- not a rebellion epic;
- not a magical court case;
- not a story where a document is accessible for convenience;
- not a story where truth automatically wins because it is true.
