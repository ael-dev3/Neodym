## Full Story Sense Check
The current Book One is about Elsbet Vale, a low-born chapel copyist at Saint Brenna's, discovering that bailiff Ruan Kest is trying to make Maura Dain's marriage vanish from the fair copy so Wren Dain can be treated as legally weaker and Nine-Candle Meadow/ferry can be controlled during flood pressure. The protagonist is Elsbet. The central human conflict is whether a poor woman whose livelihood depends on clean copying will let a profitable omission become lawful when refusing endangers her brother Tomas, Father Orlan's chapel seal, Matra Voss's candle contract, and Wren's safety.

The emotional spine is Elsbet learning that exact records are not neutral when powerful people decide which exactness counts. She wants to keep her brother safe and do her appointed work; by the end she chooses a public margin cause that preserves Maura Dain's name and blocks a clean erasure, even though it does not save everyone. The beginning sets the fair-copy trap, Tomas's bond, and the ninth candle. The middle makes proof costly through Wren's cord, Matra's slat, the ferry meadow, Orlan's key, the Glass Mile bend, and Ruan's private compromise. The ending pays off the beginning by entering Maura Dain into the fair-copy margin rather than letting absence sit there.

Essential chapters: 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, and 12. Chapter 7 is essential for route economics and thin-veil restraint but should remain watched for excursion drag. The middle makes that ending more inevitable because each proof object arrives with custody, access, and social cost.
