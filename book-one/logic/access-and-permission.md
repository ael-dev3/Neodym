# Access and permission audit

## Rough chapel register

- **Where kept:** locked in the chapel chest under the sacristy shelf.
- **Owner/custodian:** Father Orlan Rusk.
- **Guard:** key on Father Orlan's belt; chapel locked at night.
- **Who knows it exists:** everyone knows the chapel keeps records, but few know the detailed entries.
- **Why Elsbet can reach it:** she is the appointed copyist preparing the quarterly fair copy.
- **Why everyone cannot reach it:** handling the register without priestly permission is tampering.
- **Risk of access:** if Elsbet copies or conceals wrongly, she can be accused of false witness.
- **If discovered misused:** loss of post, fine, debt forfeiture, public penance.

## Quarterly fair copy

- **Where kept:** on Elsbet's copying board until sealed; then in a leather tube for court.
- **Owner/custodian:** chapel until sealed; manor after court entry.
- **Guard:** Father Orlan's seal and Elsbet's witness mark.
- **Why Elsbet can reach it:** she is making it.
- **Why everyone cannot reach it:** until sealed, it is under chapel control; after sealed, court control.
- **Risk:** the fair copy can transform a lie into an official court fact.
- **If discovered false:** Elsbet and Orlan may be punished even if Ruan pressured them.

## Manor chest copy

- **Where kept:** manor record room, in a locked chest.
- **Owner/custodian:** Lady Merrow's household, practically controlled by Ruan and Steward Harl.
- **Guard:** manor locks, armed doorward, court custom.
- **Why Elsbet cannot reach it:** she has no manor office.
- **Plot use:** its inaccessibility creates the obstacle; Elsbet must use chapel and candle proof instead.
- **Risk if accessed unlawfully:** theft accusation, imprisonment, loss of credibility.

## Candlewright receipt slats

- **Where kept:** Matra Voss's workroom, tied by quarter and candle size.
- **Owner/custodian:** Matra Voss.
- **Guard:** household control and trade memory, not formal law.
- **Why Elsbet can reach them:** only if Matra trusts her or is persuaded by Wren's danger.
- **Why everyone cannot:** they are private trade records.
- **Risk:** Ruan can dismiss them as trade marks unless matched to chapel entry.
- **If discovered:** Matra risks losing manor candle contract.

## Maura Dain's marriage cord

- **Where kept:** Wren's cottage, wrapped with Maura's best kerchief.
- **Owner/custodian:** Wren Dain.
- **Guard:** locked poor door, neighbor eyes, Wren's suspicion.
- **Why Elsbet can reach it:** Wren brings it after understanding her mother's name is being erased.
- **Why everyone cannot:** it is household property.
- **Risk:** if stolen or planted, it weakens the claim; it supports but does not prove by itself.

## Nine-Candle Meadow / ferry landing

- **Where kept:** not an object; a meadow, cottage, and ferry bank.
- **Controller:** Wren's household by custom; manor by law; Ruan by pressure.
- **Guard:** neighbors, ferry ropes, bailiff's men during dispute.
- **Why Elsbet must go there:** to see why the claim matters and verify the physical ferry lease marks.
- **Why not everyone can:** floodwater, bailiff watch, and trespass accusation.
- **Risk:** being caught there lets Ruan frame Elsbet as meddling in a property dispute.

## Glass Mile boundary stones

- **Where kept:** along the old white road above the ferry marsh.
- **Owner/custodian:** no human owner.
- **Guard:** taboo, fear, animals refusing, old custom.
- **Why Elsbet can approach:** anyone physically can, but doing so invites rumor and accusation.
- **Why everyone cannot:** social fear and practical danger keep most away.
- **Risk:** Ruan can use the taboo to discredit witnesses as curse-chasers or liars.
- **Plot function:** shapes route and property pressure, not proof.

## Wren Dain

- **Where held:** initially in her cottage; later threatened with wardship placement in Ruan's cousin's kitchen.
- **Custodian:** legally unsettled after Maura's death.
- **Why Elsbet can speak with her:** Wren comes to the chapel and Elsbet has known her through candle fees and copying work.
- **Risk:** involving Wren publicly may expose her to faster seizure.

## Rule for future chapters

If a scene needs access, the chapter guide must answer: who owns this, who guards it, why Elsbet can reach it, and what risk access creates.
