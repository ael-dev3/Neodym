# 09 - Risk register

## Plot logic risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Record procedure becomes confusing | Reader loses trust or interest | Dramatize rough book, fair copy, seal, court entry through action in Chapter 1 |
| One omitted marriage feels too small | Stakes may seem weak | Show immediate wardship removal and ferry meadow value early |
| Ruan could use easier methods | Antagonist feels artificial | Keep constraints visible: chapel rough book, public court, appeal risk, no open blood |
| Steward delay feels too convenient | Climax may feel soft | Make Harl delay only to protect procedure, not out of compassion |

## Worldbuilding risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Glass Mile becomes decorative | Aurelith layer feels pasted on | Tie it to route economics and ferry value |
| Too much institution exposition | Prose slows | Explain only when a character uses or abuses a rule |
| Old levy-bell canon leaks back | Restart loses clarity | Keep old names/plot in archive only unless explicitly reused with new logic |

## Character motivation risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Elsbet seems too passive | Copyist role could become observational | Give her choices: delay, verify, refuse seal, public challenge |
| Father Orlan becomes mere coward | Weak authority can bore readers | Make his fear institutional: losing the chapel seal harms many poor people |
| Wren becomes an object | Stakes become abstract | Give Wren necessary actions: marriage cord, refusal, hard questions |
| Ruan becomes flat villain | Human-first rule fails | Show practical road pressure and fear of account review |

## Pacing risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Verification scenes repeat | Middle sags | Each proof source must add a new cost and change available choices |
| Court climax gets too procedural | Emotional payoff weak | Keep Wren, Elsbet's debt, and Maura's name visible during procedure |

## Aurelith-overuse risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| The Glass Mile explains too much | Thin veil breaks | Never confirm what it is; no direct effects on records |
| Aurelith become moral judges | Human agency collapses | Humans cause, argue, and resolve the dispute |

## Institutional plausibility risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Elsbet's access feels too broad | Repeats old failure | She accesses only chapel materials she is assigned to copy |
| Manor chest access becomes tempting | Lazy solution risk | Keep manor chest inaccessible until court, forcing alternate proof |
| Candle proof seems too strong | Material evidence may over-solve | Candle slat corroborates; it does not decide alone |

## Coincidence risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| All evidence appears when needed | Plot feels forced | Evidence exists because of established trades: chapel, candlewright, ward household, ferry marks |
| Flood timing too convenient | Deadline feels authored | Establish seasonal flood court as normal before conflict escalates |

## Reader-confusion risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Too many names | Small story gets muddy | Keep named cast tight; add no named villagers unless functional |
| Legal terms overload | Emotional clarity suffers | Pair each term with immediate human consequence |

## Current top risk

Chapter 1 must make the mechanism clear without reading like notes. It should open with Elsbet writing the fair copy, Ruan waiting for the seal, and the contradiction visible in one specific omitted line.
