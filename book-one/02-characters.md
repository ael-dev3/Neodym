# 02 - Characters

Every named character must have a story function. No decorative names.

## Elsbet Vale

- **Role:** protagonist; low-born chapel copyist.
- **Immediate want:** finish the fair copy, earn her fee, and keep her household debt from being called.
- **Fear:** that her hand will make a lie lawful and that refusing will ruin her family.
- **Knows:** the rough register, copying procedure, candle notation, who paid which fees.
- **Misunderstands:** she thinks careful writing can stay morally neutral.
- **Can actually do:** read, copy, compare entries, delay sealing, speak as witness to her own hand.
- **Power she has:** lawful access to the rough register and fair copy.
- **Power she lacks:** manor authority, physical protection, wealth, noble backing.
- **What would make her lie:** debt threats against her household or fear of destroying the chapel's seal right.
- **What would make her betray someone:** if she believes one person's truth will harm many poor households.
- **What would make her brave:** realizing Wren will be erased through Elsbet's own handwriting.
- **Story function:** legitimate access plus moral pressure.

## Wren Dain

- **Role:** Maura Dain's twelve-year-old daughter and vulnerable claimant.
- **Want:** keep her mother's cottage, name, and marriage cord.
- **Fear:** being sent as a ward into Ruan's cousin's kitchen.
- **Knows:** household objects, her mother's words, where the marriage cord is hidden.
- **Misunderstands:** thinks adults already know the truth and are choosing not to care.
- **Can actually do:** bring the marriage cord, refuse removal, force adults to look at a living cost.
- **Power she has:** emotional truth and household memory.
- **Power she lacks:** legal standing, literacy, adult witness power.
- **Would lie for:** keeping her mother's name intact.
- **Would betray:** almost no one, but she may run if she thinks Elsbet will sign the false copy.
- **Would be brave for:** her dead mother.
- **Story function:** makes the record dispute human.

## Ruan Kest

- **Role:** antagonist; manor bailiff.
- **Want:** secure Nine-Candle Meadow's ferry landing before flood court.
- **Fear:** account review, loss of status, and exposure as a bailiff who could not deliver road income.
- **Knows:** court timing, manor chest version, Father Orlan's debt, Elsbet's debt.
- **Misunderstands:** thinks poor people will choose safety over a dead woman's name every time.
- **Can actually do:** move court schedules, threaten fines, control summons, pressure debtors, manage manor records.
- **Power he has:** procedural control and social fear.
- **Power he lacks:** lawful access to the chapel rough register and noble immunity if exposed publicly.
- **Would lie for:** status, income, and the belief that road order matters more than one child.
- **Would betray:** anyone below him if the entry stays clean.
- **Would be brave for:** his own advancement, perhaps a dependent he never discusses plainly.
- **Story function:** human corruption under institutional constraints.

## Father Orlan Rusk

- **Role:** chapel priest and custodian of the rough register.
- **Want:** keep the chapel's seal right and avoid scandal.
- **Fear:** his failing eyesight and debts will be used to strip the chapel of authority.
- **Knows:** the record custom, older entries, and what losing the seal would cost the poor.
- **Misunderstands:** thinks delay can protect everyone.
- **Can actually do:** allow Elsbet access, seal or refuse the fair copy, call limited chapel witness.
- **Power he has:** seal authority.
- **Power he lacks:** courage against manor pressure and clear eyesight.
- **Would lie for:** preserving the chapel as an institution.
- **Would betray:** Elsbet by asking her to carry a burden he should carry.
- **Would be brave for:** the fear that all chapel records will become useless if this lie passes.
- **Story function:** weak authority with real stakes.

## Matra Voss

- **Role:** candlewright, widow, and material witness.
- **Want:** keep her candle contract and avoid manor punishment.
- **Fear:** losing trade and becoming dependent on charity.
- **Knows:** candle payments, wax tallies, who bought Maura's ninth candle.
- **Misunderstands:** believes trade marks cannot matter against sealed records.
- **Can actually do:** produce receipt slats, testify to a candle count, hide or reveal proof.
- **Power she has:** material corroboration.
- **Power she lacks:** official standing.
- **Would lie for:** survival of her trade.
- **Would betray:** a friend under threat, then hate herself for it.
- **Would be brave for:** another widow's name.
- **Story function:** proof outside official documents.

## Harl Brey

- **Role:** manor steward and flood court presider.
- **Want:** clean entries, no reversible decisions, no scandal reaching Lady Merrow.
- **Fear:** procedural dirt that makes him look incompetent.
- **Knows:** court forms, appeal risks, and how bailiffs bend timing.
- **Misunderstands:** thinks a delay is neutral when people are being harmed.
- **Can actually do:** enter, reject, or delay the fair copy and wardship order.
- **Power he has:** formal court decision.
- **Power he lacks:** moral imagination and local affection.
- **Would lie for:** administrative convenience.
- **Would betray:** Ruan if Ruan becomes a liability.
- **Would be brave for:** his own clean record, not justice.
- **Story function:** allows a narrow win without making the system benevolent.

## Tomas Vale

- **Role:** Elsbet's younger brother; household debt pressure.
- **Want:** avoid being bound to mill labor for debt.
- **Fear:** that Elsbet's honesty will cost him his body.
- **Knows:** how debts feel, not how records work.
- **Misunderstands:** thinks a signature is only ink.
- **Can actually do:** pressure Elsbet emotionally, carry a message, make the cost visible.
- **Power he has:** family love as leverage.
- **Power he lacks:** money, age, authority.
- **Would lie for:** Elsbet.
- **Would betray:** no one intentionally, but panic may make him speak to the wrong person.
- **Would be brave for:** keeping Elsbet from taking all blame.
- **Story function:** makes the moral dilemma costly on both sides.

## Maura Dain

- **Role:** dead widow whose name is being erased.
- **Want:** not active; represented through records, Wren, and household remnants.
- **Fear:** not active.
- **Knows:** not active.
- **Story function:** the dead person whose legal identity becomes the battlefield.

## Naming rule

Do not add named villagers unless they provide proof, pressure, danger, or consequence.
