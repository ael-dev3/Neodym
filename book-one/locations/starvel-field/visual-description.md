# Starvel Field — Visual Description

## Core Visual Identity
Pale field above the old road with white stones along the lower edge; maps bend around it.

## Shapes and Layout
Keep the layout readable as a human working place: roads, doors, thresholds, storage, fields, or stones should imply what people do there.

## Materials
Wool, mud, wet stone, road dust, old wood, iron, grain, ash, and cheap leather as appropriate.

## Weather / Light
Often seen through rain, mist, or far blue-white weather; never explained.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Quiet too large for the valley, horses balking, wind changed by distance, wet grass no one cuts.

## Human Activity
People avoid it, lower banners near it, argue about it, and use its taboo for human politics.

## Color Palette
Grounded rural colors: mud brown, rain gray, barley gold, ash black, wool cream, wet stone.

## Symbolic Motif
white stones under rain

## Image Generation Prompt
Grounded high-fantasy rural location concept art of Starvel Field, forbidden upland / Aurelith-adjacent boundary, in the world of Neodym Book One, human-first, tactile mud and wool realism, restrained palette, rain and road pressure, no modern technology, no generic elven city, no lore-explainer composition.

## Negative Prompt
No sci-fi screens, no neon, no clean castle fantasy gloss, no elves explaining the scene, no massive battle, no modern roads, no franchise imitation.
