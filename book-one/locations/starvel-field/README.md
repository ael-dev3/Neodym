# Starvel Field

## Quick Summary
Starvel Field is a forbidden upland / Aurelith-adjacent boundary in Book One. Starts as distant taboo; becomes the stage where human lies cannot hide behind banners.

## Story Function
People avoid it, lower banners near it, argue about it, and use its taboo for human politics.
