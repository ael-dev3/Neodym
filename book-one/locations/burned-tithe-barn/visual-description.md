# Burned Tithe Barn — Visual Description

## Core Visual Identity
Ash heap, burned beams, dead cart horses nearby or invoked, soot, boot print, hidden letter.

## Shapes and Layout
Keep the layout readable as a human working place: roads, doors, thresholds, storage, fields, or stones should imply what people do there.

## Materials
Wool, mud, wet stone, road dust, old wood, iron, grain, ash, and cheap leather as appropriate.

## Weather / Light
Rain wakes blood and smears ash; smoke smell lingers after flame.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Wet ash, char, horsehair, boot leather, hot-cold smoke.

## Human Activity
Used to justify levy, hide crime, and recover proof.

## Color Palette
Grounded rural colors: mud brown, rain gray, barley gold, ash black, wool cream, wet stone.

## Symbolic Motif
ash in boot leather

## Image Generation Prompt
Grounded high-fantasy rural location concept art of Burned Tithe Barn, burned barn and false-cause evidence site, in the world of Neodym Book One, human-first, tactile mud and wool realism, restrained palette, rain and road pressure, no modern technology, no generic elven city, no lore-explainer composition.

## Negative Prompt
No sci-fi screens, no neon, no clean castle fantasy gloss, no elves explaining the scene, no massive battle, no modern roads, no franchise imitation.
