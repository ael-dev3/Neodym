# Saint Orra's Chapel — Visual Description

## Core Visual Identity
Small stone building, west door, poor burial ground, candle racks, locked chests, old witness marks.

## Shapes and Layout
Keep the layout readable as a human working place: roads, doors, thresholds, storage, fields, or stones should imply what people do there.

## Materials
Wool, mud, wet stone, road dust, old wood, iron, grain, ash, and cheap leather as appropriate.

## Weather / Light
Cold stone and damp threshold, with candle heat that never reaches the corners.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Wax, old wool, wet stone, dust in registers, iron keys.

## Human Activity
People swear oaths, bury poor dead, ask for quiet bargains, and seek forms power might respect.

## Color Palette
Grounded rural colors: mud brown, rain gray, barley gold, ash black, wool cream, wet stone.

## Symbolic Motif
west door, wax, keys

## Image Generation Prompt
Grounded high-fantasy rural location concept art of Saint Orra's Chapel, small stone chapel and witness place, in the world of Neodym Book One, human-first, tactile mud and wool realism, restrained palette, rain and road pressure, no modern technology, no generic elven city, no lore-explainer composition.

## Negative Prompt
No sci-fi screens, no neon, no clean castle fantasy gloss, no elves explaining the scene, no massive battle, no modern roads, no franchise imitation.
