# Bramble Gate Road — Visual Description

## Core Visual Identity
Road out toward Bramble Gate, watched by riders, carts, tolls, and rumors of safer side ways.

## Shapes and Layout
Keep the layout readable as a human working place: roads, doors, thresholds, storage, fields, or stones should imply what people do there.

## Materials
Wool, mud, wet stone, road dust, old wood, iron, grain, ash, and cheap leather as appropriate.

## Weather / Light
Mud in rain, dust in dry wind, exposed to patrols.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Hooves, wheel ruts, wet leather, road dust, cold iron at belts.

## Human Activity
Carries soldiers, grain, market carts, letters, and threats.

## Color Palette
Grounded rural colors: mud brown, rain gray, barley gold, ash black, wool cream, wet stone.

## Symbolic Motif
ruts leading toward a bronze boar

## Image Generation Prompt
Grounded high-fantasy rural location concept art of Bramble Gate Road, watched road under Lord Venn pressure, in the world of Neodym Book One, human-first, tactile mud and wool realism, restrained palette, rain and road pressure, no modern technology, no generic elven city, no lore-explainer composition.

## Negative Prompt
No sci-fi screens, no neon, no clean castle fantasy gloss, no elves explaining the scene, no massive battle, no modern roads, no franchise imitation.
