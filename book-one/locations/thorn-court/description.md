A small court place with old benches, thorn hedges or oath-thorns, frightened clerks, and little grandeur.

## Weather / Seasonal Feel
Damp, crowded, and sour with delayed business.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Wet wool, old ink, ale breath, hedge leaves, scraped benches.

## Human Use

## Political Control
Human local legal network vulnerable to noble pressure.

## Social Meaning
A hoped-for lawful route that fails Nera.

## Local Customs
Witness forms, oath seating, public readings.

## Dangers
Delay, betrayal, bought silence, legal trap.

## Secrets
May already be compromised before Nera arrives.
