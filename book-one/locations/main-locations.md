## Mentioned-only places

- **Wickerlow Hearthmarch:** wider rural region around Low Wicker.
- **Dun Mere:** nearby place where a twelve-year-old was taken for carrying arrows; use sparingly as proof of levy cruelty.
- **Ashmere:** alleged enemy source blamed for the tithe barn fire; keep as human border-war pressure, not a lore detour.
- **Bramble Gate:** Lord Venn’s power-center or roadward base, mostly off-page in Book One.
