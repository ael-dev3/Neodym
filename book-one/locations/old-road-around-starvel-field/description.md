# Old Road Around Starvel Field — Description

## Type of Place
older chapel way / boundary-skirt road.

## First Appearance
Chapter 4 planned — `book-one/chapter-guides/chapter-04-saint-orras-west-door.md`.

## Later Appearances
Chapters 6, 7, 10, 12 planned.

## Physical Description
Narrower old route bending around white stones and bad ground, half-kept by memory more than maps.

## Weather / Seasonal Feel
Rain makes it slick; mist makes distances uncertain.

## Smell / Sound / Texture
Thorn, wet grass, stone under mud, horse breath, quiet beyond the stones.

## Human Use
Used by those avoiding watched roads, old chapel traffic, smugglers, and desperate witnesses.

## Political Control
No clean control; local knowledge and taboo govern it.

## Social Meaning
A way out that is also a warning.

## Local Customs
Lower banners, avoid loud claims, do not cut across the field.

## Dangers
Patrols, getting lost, being accused of taboo-breaking, horse panic.

## Secrets
Old route knowledge can beat the watched road.
