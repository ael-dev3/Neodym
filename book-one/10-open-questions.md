# 10 — Open Questions

## Owner-level story decisions

1. Which title feels strongest: **The Bell on Starvel Hill**, **The Road That Hummed Back**, or **The Child the Ledger Named**?
2. Should Nera remain the single POV for the whole novella, or should the story include short human account-book fragments between chapters?
3. Is Pell the right age at 12, or should he be slightly older to carry more agency?
4. Should the final result restrict the Bridge-Bell Rhyme, or should it survive in a more hidden way with no official permission at all?
5. Should Nera leave Low Wicker at the end, or stay and become the valley's more dangerous memory keeper?
6. How strange should the Silver Rider appear: almost human, clearly otherworldly, or barely visible enough to stay more mythic?
7. Should Bruk be Cairnkin, or would Ael prefer Book One to stay almost entirely human except for distant Aurelith pressure?

## Craft questions for future improvement passes

1. Does Osric's motive feel sympathetic enough without weakening the danger he creates?
2. Does Osa's belief in order feel emotionally grounded, or does she need a sharper personal history?
3. Can the Bridge-Bell Rhyme gain more concrete lines without becoming too poetic or too magical?
4. Is the mirror-pillar too useful in the climax, and should Nera's proof depend more on human testimony and risk?
5. Are there enough moments of humor and warmth before the legal pressure tightens?
6. Does the ending win enough for the reader without feeling like a larger rebellion has begun?
7. Does Starvel Hill feel mysterious rather than vague?
