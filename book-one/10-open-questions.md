# 10 - Open questions

These are steering hooks, not blockers. If no owner answer exists, choose the option that makes Book One a sharper human story with subtler Aurelith pressure.

## Title

1. Keep **The Field Where No Banners Go**?
2. Use **The Ash Road Letter** if the messenger plot becomes dominant?
3. Use **The Boy on the Levy Roll** if Pell's stakes should be centered harder?

## Story shape

1. Should Nera reach an actual minor court, or should the court fail and force the climax back to Low Wicker?
2. Is Joryn redeemable enough to testify, or should Bruk become the decisive witness?
3. How much direct violence should Chapter 10 allow while staying novella-scale?
4. Should Osric survive socially, be punished narrowly, or escape cleanly?

## Aurelith thin veil

1. Should Starvel Field ever show blue-white light in Book One, or stay entirely human-interpreted?
2. Should the white stones react at all, or should their power be only in human behavior?
3. Is one Level 3 relic too much for this book?

## Character

1. Is Nera still the right protagonist name?
2. Should Pell be thirteen, twelve, or fourteen for levy plausibility?
3. Should Osa be Nera's aunt by blood or only by work-family?
