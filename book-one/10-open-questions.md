# 10 - Open questions

Owner feedback overrides these.

## Craft questions

1. Is **The Ninth Candle** the right title, or should the title foreground the ferry meadow or fair copy?
2. Should Elsbet's household debt involve her brother Tomas, her father's grave fee, or both?
3. Should Father Orlan redeem himself by refusing the seal publicly, or stay weak and let Elsbet carry the public risk?
4. Should Ruan's sympathetic pressure be account review, family ransom, road obligation, or a mix?
5. How visible should the Glass Mile be before Chapter 7?
6. Should the final win be chapel wardship until harvest or a delayed appeal to a deanery?
7. How harsh should Elsbet's personal cost be: job loss, debt called, exile, or coerced service?

## Current default answers

1. Keep **The Ninth Candle** for now.
2. Use Tomas and household debt because it makes the dilemma immediate.
3. Father Orlan helps but does not become the hero.
4. Ruan's pressure is account review and road obligation; avoid overcomplicating with family ransom unless needed.
5. Keep the Glass Mile mostly distant until the route/ferry economics require it.
6. Use chapel wardship until harvest for a narrow, local win.
7. Elsbet loses safe invisibility, not necessarily her home in Book One.
