# 09 — Visual Prompts

These prompts are for small-scale Book One development only. Keep visuals grounded, rural, muddy, and human-centered, with Aurelith presence as distant cold traces.

## Prompt 01 — Low Wicker valley

**Subject:** Low Wicker, a small sheep valley beneath a forbidden silver-lit hill.

**Composition:** Wide rural establishing shot from a muddy road, village roofs and sheep pens in the foreground, Saint Orra's bridge crossing a brown river, Starvel Hill distant in mist with faint blue-silver light above the slope.

**Materials:** Fieldstone cottages, thatch, slate patches, wool cloaks, wooden carts, wet fences, clay road, old chapel bell tower.

**Lighting:** Rainy morning, warm hearth smoke near the village, cold silver-blue glow far above the hill.

**Mood:** Intimate high fantasy, poor and lived-in, beautiful but oppressed.

**Worldbuilding detail:** Human road bends away from a cleaner sealed road no one walks.

**Negative prompt:** Avoid modern technology, screens, robots, guns, neon cyberpunk, generic sci-fi, plastic armor, Tolkien-copy elves, anime style, large battle scenes, giant futuristic cities, overexplained magic, clean superhero poster composition.

## Prompt 02 — Nera Brindle portrait

**Subject:** Nera Brindle, low-born village letter-reader and toll scribe.

**Composition:** Three-quarter portrait at a bakehouse table, one hand on a toll ledger, the other holding a wax-cutting knife; flour on sleeve, rain-dark cloak over simple wool dress.

**Materials:** Worn parchment, bread crusts, iron knife, wool, linen apron, cracked wooden table, red-brown wax seals.

**Lighting:** Warm oven glow from one side, gray daylight through small window, faint cold reflection from a distant mirror token.

**Mood:** Practical, wary, stubborn, emotionally guarded.

**Worldbuilding detail:** A small Name-Iron registry tag hangs near the ledger but is not centered.

**Negative prompt:** Avoid modern technology, screens, robots, guns, neon cyberpunk, generic sci-fi, plastic armor, Tolkien-copy elves, anime style, large battle scenes, giant futuristic cities, overexplained magic, clean superhero poster composition.

## Prompt 11 — The Silver Rider at a distance
