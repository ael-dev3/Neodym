# 06 — Geography

## Cyr Veyr, the Crescent Archive

- **Description:** Aurelith capital carved into a crescent mountain of black stone. Silver towers grow like frozen roots around Neodym veins. Bridges cross empty air. Moon Vaults descend for miles.
- **Political control:** direct Quiet Concord rule.
- **Visual identity:** black rock, pale bridges, blue-silver glow, still water courts, mirror balconies.
- **Factions:** Quiet Concord, Moon Archivists, Orrery Hands, Mercy Heresy.
- **Story use:** center of power, impossible heist, final revelation.
- **Secrets:** the Unlit Seat meets under the oldest vault, where a human name has been cut from every inscription.

## Selachor, City of Measured Bread

- **Description:** a model human province capital with clean canals, public bakeries, schools, and mirror wells in every square.
- **Control:** Sealed House Vale under Aurelith supervision.
- **Visual identity:** warm clay houses forced into symmetrical streets, silver census gates, flour dust, obedient murals.
- **Factions:** Sealed Houses, Province Watch, Emberwake bakers.
- **Story use:** shows why many humans accept Concord rule.
- **Secrets:** bread stamps carry hidden rebel routes.

## Rustvale and the Hollow Moon Mines

- **Description:** industrial mountain valleys where Cairnkin and human laborers mine Neodym under dangerous wards.
- **Control:** Ledger Houses and Ivory Quiet.
- **Visual identity:** rust water, basalt lifts, glowing ore dust, respirator veils, worker shrines.
- **Factions:** Cairnkin unions, mine priests, black-market ore cutters.
- **Story use:** class conflict, collapse, discovery of ancient truth.
- **Secrets:** the deepest seam contains pre-Aurelith tools.

## Morrowfen

- **Description:** drowned fungal forest where root-minds store living memory and paths change according to grief.
- **Control:** Thallor councils; nominally outside Concord law.
- **Visual identity:** black water, luminous spores, root bridges, mushroom towers, slow voices.
- **Factions:** Thallor elders, Emberwake fugitives, Aurelith envoys.
- **Story use:** forbidden history, refuge, moral test of patience.
- **Secrets:** the fen remembers the first Aurelith as frightened rather than divine.

## Gravewind Marches

- **Description:** border grasslands haunted by bone beasts, old battle winds, and Vharakul clan towers.
- **Control:** Vharakul clans under treaty.
- **Visual identity:** tall silver grass, storm skies, bone signal towers, red oath flags.
- **Factions:** Vharakul auxiliaries, monster hunters, rebel caravans.
- **Story use:** war, loyalty conflict, chase sequences.
- **Secrets:** monsters may be remnants of Sky Fever victims, not natural beasts.

## The Unnumbered Sea

- **Description:** moonlit sea where predictions blur and Nacren reef cities hide under storm glass.
- **Control:** Nacren tide courts, pirates, and contested ports.
- **Visual identity:** pearl reefs, dark water, shell gates, floating lantern nets, salt mist.
- **Factions:** Nacren diplomats, smugglers, Aurelith naval envoys.
- **Story use:** escape, diplomacy, lost moon fragment.
- **Secrets:** Neodym behaves differently under deep salt water, as if remembering another sky.

## The Blank Kilns

- **Description:** white ash ruins of a city that memory cannot hold. Maps blur. Names vanish from speech.
- **Control:** none stable; Eirrem courts drift through it.
- **Visual identity:** white dust, melted glass, floating wax seals, empty masks, silent bells.
- **Factions:** Eirrem, Ash Synod, forbidden scholars.
- **Story use:** horror, testimony, lost truth.
- **Secrets:** a soul-ledger survived beneath the central kiln.

## Cinder Crowns

- **Description:** volcanic mountain chain where Dravyr heartfire sanctuaries refine Neodym.
- **Control:** Aurelith extraction temples guarded by Ivory Quiet.
- **Visual identity:** black lava, ember fissures, chain halos, furnace shrines, gold smoke.
- **Factions:** Dravyr remnants, Aurelith refiners, human pilgrims.
- **Story use:** liberation versus disaster dilemma.
- **Secrets:** one Dravyr heart still dreams of flight and knows how to melt identity seals.

## Glassmeadow

- **Description:** wealthy collaborator countryside of vineyards, mirrored schools, and ornamental canals.
- **Control:** Sealed Houses and Aurelith tutors.
- **Visual identity:** green fields trimmed into geometry, white villas, silver trellises, anxious luxury.
- **Factions:** young collaborators, reformist tutors, quiet rebel servants.
- **Story use:** moral complexity, seduction by comfort.
- **Secrets:** several Sealed heirs are secretly marked Red.

## The Redroot Warrens

- **Description:** rebel safe zone beneath old human catacombs and living roots stained by iron-rich water.
- **Control:** Emberwake cells.
- **Visual identity:** red roots, candle tunnels, quilt maps, bone flutes, hidden presses.
- **Factions:** Bloodsong teachers, memory smugglers, Ash Synod radicals.
- **Story use:** rebel politics, training, betrayal.
- **Secrets:** a radical faction plans to poison an archive aqueduct.

## The Star Roads

- **Description:** ancient elevated roads built during the Age of Guidance, lined with ghost-lanterns and mirror tolls.
- **Control:** Concord patrols, Ledger Houses, smugglers.
- **Visual identity:** pale stone causeways, silver milestones, roadside shrines, hidden chalk marks.
- **Factions:** merchants, Province Watch, Caltrow Kith.
- **Story use:** travel, ambush, social cross-section.
- **Secrets:** some road segments predate the Aurelith and point toward erased cities.

## Jester Markets

- **Description:** temporary markets under bridges, ruins, or festival tents where Caltrow Kith trade impossible favors.
- **Control:** Caltrow treaty custom.
- **Visual identity:** patchwork awnings, cracked bells, masks, chalk doors, lantern smoke.
- **Factions:** Caltrow brokers, memory smugglers, spies, desperate parents.
- **Story use:** black market, comic relief, dangerous bargain.
- **Secrets:** one market can sell a day in which the Mirror Choir forgets you.

## Trade routes

- **Silver Road:** Neodym from Rustvale to Cyr Veyr.
- **Bread Road:** grain from Hearthmarch to model cities.
- **Salt Veil Route:** Nacren sea-smuggling path.
- **Root Path:** Thallor-guided refugee trail through Morrowfen.
- **Oath Road:** Vharakul border military road.

## Visual notes

- Geography should contrast Aurelith symmetry with human improvisation and wild prediction-resistant regions.
- Maps should show official borders and unofficial routes separately.
- Every major region should include signs of both survival and control.
