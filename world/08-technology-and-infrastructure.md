# 08 — Technology and Infrastructure

This file translates surveillance, identity, class systems, black markets, and predictive governance into fantasy language. In-world language should remain mythic: mirrors, seals, vaults, ledgers, songs, and moonlit archives rather than modern terms.

## Beautiful oppressive infrastructure

The Aurelith do not rule primarily through crude occupation. They rule through things people need:

- aqueducts that sing impurities out of water;
- grain ledgers that prevent famine;
- plague veils over crowded cities;
- silver bridges that shorten trade routes;
- moon clocks that regulate planting;
- archive courts that settle inheritance disputes;
- public healers who can remove pain in exchange for memory tithe.

The cage is functional, graceful, and often beloved.

## Surveillance magic: The Mirror Choir

The Mirror Choir consists of still-water basins, black mirrors, polished Neodym inlays, listening statues, census songs, and human informants. It watches for patterns of unrest rather than every single act. A village becomes suspect when songs change, bread purchases shift, children vanish from school, or funeral attendance exceeds prediction.

Daily experience:

- people lower their voices near public wells;
- children learn approved facial calm;
- lovers meet in bakeries because ovens distort mirror-water;
- mourners fear grief will mark them as unstable;
- workers joke in rhythms that hide meaning.

## Identity seals: Name-Irons

Name-Irons bind a person to public records. They appear as bracelets, throat rings, clay tablets, stamped papers, or silver-thread tattoos depending on class. A seal may record name, family, province, guild, travel rights, risk marks, debts, and permitted rituals.

The wealthy wear elegant seals. The poor wear crude ones that chafe. Prisoners carry hot black seals that stain the skin.

## Memory tracking

Memory is taxed through:

- **Last Witness fees:** payment to preserve a dying person's testimony.
- **Apprentice imprinting:** workers surrender technique memories to guild vaults.
- **Trial extraction:** courts pull sensory fragments as evidence.
- **Grief tithe:** families offer one memory of the dead in exchange for funeral permits.

Black-market memory smugglers hide memories in wax teeth, bread yeast, shell knots, and prayer beads.

## Predictive law

Orrery Courts calculate likely harm before it occurs. People can be restricted for predicted crimes, relocated for predicted unrest, or denied marriage because a union forecasts dangerous offspring. The Concord calls this mercy. Humans call it punishment for unlived lives.

## Magical class systems

Class is marked by proximity to clean magic:

- High Aurelith: direct archive access.
- Sealed Houses: refined Name-Irons and private tutors.
- Guild citizens: work seals and travel permits.
- Common registered humans: bread rights and local law.
- Restricted gifted: monitored, relocated, or trained under supervision.
- Unsealed: illegal, invisible, vulnerable.
- Eirrem: often not legally persons.

## Black markets

- **Dream thieves:** steal nightmares before mirrors can read them.
- **Memory smugglers:** move forbidden histories and personal memories.
- **Seal cutters:** alter Name-Irons.
- **Candle printers:** hide messages in wax impurities.
- **Song brokers:** sell melodies that carry meaning only when sung off-tempo.
- **Caltrow misrulers:** create charms that make patrols arrive one minute late.

## Rebel communication

The Emberwake communicates through:

- funeral drums;
- quilt maps;
- bread stamps;
- red thread knots;
- wrong notes in hymns;
- Nacren shell codes;
- Thallor spore marks;
- Caltrow chalk doors.

A secure message is called a **closed song**. It can be opened only by someone who knows the grief or joke hidden inside it.

## Magical equivalents of common oppressive tools

- **Surveillance cameras:** mirror wells, listening saints, silver rain chains.
- **Biometric identity:** Name-Irons, throat seals, blood-thread signatures, face-memory in court mirrors.
- **Social credit:** bread warmth marks, road permissions, school privileges, marriage approvals, public trust colors on seals.
- **Predictive policing:** Orrery warrants and Mercy patrols sent before unrest occurs.
- **Encrypted communication:** closed songs, salt knots, quilt maps, ash prayers, Caltrow misrule riddles.
- **Hacking:** seal-cutting, mirror-clouding, ledger-spoiling, name-swapping with the consenting dead.
- **Data centers translated into fantasy:** Moon Vaults, crystal groves, archive-temples, star-ledger cloisters, silver libraries.

## Forbidden artifacts

- mirror-clouding ash;
- unregistered Neodym flakes;
- blank Name-Iron bracelets;
- Hearth Canticle pages;
- soul-ledger needles;
- Caltrow bells that confuse path records;
- Nacren salt masks;
- Dravyr ember keys.

## Visual notes

- Avoid modern screens, plastic, neon signage, and industrial sci-fi shapes.
- Use ghost-lanterns, rune-light, moon-glass, mirror-water, silver mist, thread, wax, bone, cloth, and stone.
- Oppression should look clean from afar and intimate up close: a beautiful seal cutting into a child's wrist.
