# 01 - Cosmology

## Human cosmology first

Most humans in Edrath do not have a single agreed map of creation. Villages keep local saints. Kings sponsor official temple histories. Border folk tell older stories. Monasteries copy star charts that contradict each other in the margins.

The Aurelith do not provide a clear public answer. Their silence leaves room for human faith, fraud, fear, and poetry.

## Common human beliefs

- The world was sung, hammered, birthed, drowned, or stolen depending on region.
- Mountains are old witnesses.
- Rivers remember names better than courts do.
- Some stars are dead kings, some are watchfires, and some are holes through which elder powers look down.
- Aurelith lights are interpreted as omens by people who do not agree what an omen is.

## Aurelith distance

Aurelith works sometimes ignore weather, distance, or decay in ways that disturb human priests. They do not explain whether this is magic, craft, prayer, or something with no human category.

## Neodym and wonder

Neodym may hold memory, oath, or motion, but those are human guesses. For Book One, it should remain a word attached to rare Aurelith works and old fear, not a solved theory.

## Open questions

- Which faith dominates the Wickerlow Hearthmarch?
- Which human sect treats the Aurelith as angels, and which treats them as soulless elder folk?
- What cosmological claim has caused the bloodiest human heresy trial?
