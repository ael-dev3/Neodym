# 07 — Religion and Myth

## Official religion: The Doctrine of Continuance

The Aurelith teach that creation survives only through remembered order. The Doctrine of Continuance honors the First Stillness, the sacred duty of memory, and the moral obligation to prevent catastrophic freedom. Its priests, the **Still Cantors**, do not shout sermons. They sing legal harmonies in courts, schools, hospitals, and census squares.

Core beliefs:

- Memory is holier than desire.
- Continuity outranks individual will.
- Beauty reveals moral alignment.
- Mercy may require restraint.
- Mortals are precious because they are temporary, but dangerous because they forget.

## Approved human saints

The Concord permits saints who reinforce obedience:

- **Saint Arven of the Last Granary:** gave all grain to prevent war; used to teach sacrifice for order.
- **Saint Melra of Closed Hands:** surrendered forbidden magic; used in Measure Day rites.
- **Saint Odrin the Bridgebuilder:** collaborated with Aurelith engineers; beloved by workers.

Underground versions of these saints often reverse the lesson.

## Forbidden human faiths

### The Laughing Flame

A hearth faith teaching that creation began in change, not stillness. Rituals use dancing, cracked bowls, red candles, and improvised prayer. Its central sacrament is a story told differently every time.

### The Ash Mother

A funerary faith preserving the dignity of death against memory extraction. Followers believe the dead must be allowed to become mystery. They sabotage soul-ledgers and hide bodies from archive collectors.

### Meyr the Edited God

A suppressed cult of a god removed from official scripture. Meyr is patron of grief, forgotten names, and memory that refuses to serve power. Meyr's symbol is a silver bone wrapped in red thread.

## Cults of Neodym

### The Bright Vein

Humans and Cairnkin who worship raw Neodym as divine truth. They seek visions through controlled poisoning. Some become prophets; many become broken.

### The Final Clarifiers

An Aurelith extremist theology holding that all suffering comes from unclarified mortal contradiction. They want to transform humanity into stable memory-bearing forms.

### The Ash Synod

Human zealots who believe Neodym is the shackle of the world. They want all mines, archives, and mirror works destroyed, even if millions die from the collapse.

## Other races' spiritual systems

- **Cairnkin:** ancestor stones, tool burials, and air-oaths sworn before tunnel mouths.
- **Vharakul:** oath scars, battle poetry, and adoption rites stronger than blood.
- **Thallor:** decomposition as sacrament; truth must rot into nourishment.
- **Dravyr:** sky memory, volcanic rebirth, and riddles as sacred law.
- **Eirrem:** mourning courts seeking either restored identity or final death.
- **Nacren:** tide law, shell saints, and dreams entrusted to the moon.
- **Caltrow Kith:** sacred tricks that puncture the arrogance of certainty.

## Conflicting prophecies

- **The Silver Peace:** official prophecy that the Concord must rule until human desire cools.
- **The Ember Crown:** rebel prophecy that a mortal song will make silver kneel.
- **The Root Warning:** Thallor vision that a perfect archive becomes a world-sized tomb.
- **The Salt Omen:** Nacren prophecy of a moon fragment rising from the sea.
- **The Ninth Mercy:** whispered Aurelith prophecy that the Unlit Seat will choose between memory and life.

## Holy texts and heresies

- **The Book of Still Mercies:** official Aurelith scripture-law.
- **The Hearth Canticles:** forbidden human oral cycle.
- **The Spores of Before:** Thallor memory rite, never written.
- **The Ash Ledger:** list of names stolen from death, copied by underground priests.
- **The Broken Orrery Sermons:** Aurelith reformist texts arguing that love without risk is possession.

## Rituals

- **Measure Day:** child testing under mirrors.
- **Census Hymn:** public identity update.
- **Ash Wedding:** human rebel marriage where vows are sung over burnt bread.
- **Memory Release:** illegal rite returning a stored memory to the grieving.
- **Clarification Vigil:** Aurelith reproduction ceremony.
- **Salt Unnaming:** Nacren ritual to hide a fugitive from land-bound seals.

## Symbols

- Aurelith: crescent mirror crossed by three silver threads.
- Emberwake: red thread tied around a burnt grain stalk.
- Ash Mother: black bowl of white ash.
- Meyr: silver bone wrapped in red thread.
- Bright Vein: blue-silver eye inside cracked stone.
- Thallor: root circle breaking open.

## Visual notes

- Religion should feel embodied: ash on hands, songs in courts, children under silver measuring rods, saints painted beneath official murals.
- Holy objects should be tactile and worn, not abstract icons alone.

## Open questions

- Was Meyr truly edited out, or invented by rebels as a useful absence?
- Which approved saint secretly founded the Emberwake?
- Can Still Cantors become rebels without losing their faith?
- Is the Ninth Mercy a salvation prophecy or an extermination order?
