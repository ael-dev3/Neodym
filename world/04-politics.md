# 04 - Politics

## Human politics first

Most human lands govern themselves. They do it badly, beautifully, violently, and stubbornly.

Edrath has kings, border lords, city leagues, abbeys, free towns, clan holds, military orders, toll courts, marriage alliances, succession crises, peasant risings, religious trials, ransom customs, and old grudges that outlive the people who started them.

The Aurelith do not administer ordinary human villages. A local bailiff is usually a human problem. So is a cruel tax, a false charge, a burned field, or a boy taken for war.

## Human kingdoms

Human kingdoms should feel different by law, terrain, faith, and economy.

Useful political pressures:

- disputed borders
- lords using war to cancel debt
- marriage treaties that doom children
- toll roads worth killing for
- abbeys that shelter fugitives for a fee
- market towns balancing rival banners
- old nobility claiming Aurelith contact as prestige
- commoners who know noble myths are lies but cannot say so safely

## Aurelith diplomacy

Aurelith contact at court level is rare. A king may spend a lifetime preparing for an envoy who never arrives. A single message, silence, or refusal can change a war because humans project meaning onto it.

The Aurelith do not negotiate like human states. They may preserve a roadless territory, refuse a border crossing, accept a tribute without comment, or ignore a crown entirely.

## False legitimacy

Human elites often use the Aurelith as political theater.

- A noble house claims an ancestor spoke with the elder folk.
- A king calls a border taboo an ancient treaty when nobody can read the stone.
- A priest declares a rival line cursed by Aurelith silence.
- A merchant league sells relic dust that is probably chalk.

The Aurelith may not care enough to correct the lie.

## Contact levels in politics

- Level 0: local politics with no Aurelith relevance.
- Level 1: myths and claims used for legitimacy.
- Level 2: borders and forbidden zones shape war routes.
- Level 3: a relic becomes a diplomatic crisis.
- Level 4: rare envoy contact with high rulers.
- Level 5: direct intervention, reserved for major saga turns.
