# Neodym Audio

Atmosphere references and source audio for the project.

## Main theme music

- [`neodym-main-theme-i.mp3`](neodym-main-theme-i.mp3) — main theme music, take I. Approx. 3:39.
- [`neodym-main-theme-ii.mp3`](neodym-main-theme-ii.mp3) — main theme music, take II. Approx. 2:39.

Use these as tonal anchors while writing or designing Neodym scenes: human-scale, sacred-haunted, restrained, and atmospheric rather than heroic fantasy bombast.

## Audio direction

- Keep narration intimate and human-first.
- Let silence, breath, room tone, wind, candle, cloth, rain, and distant metal do real work.
- Treat Aurelith/veil moments as pressure shifts and thin harmonics, not obvious magic stingers.
- Keep war textures tired, muted, and physical rather than triumphant.
