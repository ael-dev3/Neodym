# Faction visual prompts

## House Venn levy riders

Prompt: Human border-house riders in rain-dark cloaks, bronze boar pins, practical spears, muddy horses, lowered banner, hard faces of men paid to take grain and names.

## Low Wicker villagers

Prompt: Rural villagers in patched wool gathered outside a bakehouse, bakers, shepherds, widows, boys trying to look older, practical fear under rain.

## Chapel witnesses of Saint Orra

Prompt: Poor chapel witnesses with candles and road dust, plain wool, small iron keys, parchment, old threshold marks, human faith under pressure.

## Cairnkin road workers

Prompt: Small group of Cairnkin stonemenders repairing a bridge footing while human villagers watch with need and mistrust, stone tools, wet riverbank, no caricature.
