# Faction and institution visual prompts

## Chapel record keepers

Prompt: Rural chapel record-keeping as a human institution: rough register, fair-copy sheets, seal wax, candle stubs, poor villagers waiting with fees, a tired priest and low-born copyist, grounded high fantasy realism.

Negative prompt: bureaucratic office, modern filing cabinets, magical archive, elven administration.

## Manor bailiff's office

Prompt: Human manor authority at village scale: bailiff's leather gloves, court summons, rent tallies, debt tokens, ferry map, locked chest, practical coercion without splendor.

Negative prompt: emperor throne room, demon army, modern police station, glowing surveillance.

## Ferry households

Prompt: Poor ferry households beside a swollen river, ropes, planks, wet wool, toll bowl, patched cottage walls, children watching carts wait for crossing, human economy under flood pressure.

Negative prompt: grand harbor, heroic naval scene, modern dock, fantasy airships.

## Candlewright trade

Prompt: Widow candlewright guild-of-one, wax slats, moulds, tallow, wicks, trade marks, cheap contracts, hands burned by work, intimate human craft.

Negative prompt: magic potion shop, luxury candle boutique, steampunk machinery.
