# Peoples visual prompts

## Humans of Wickerlow

Prompt: Human sheep-valley villagers, patched wool, leather belts, flour dust, rain cloaks, tired eyes, practical tools, no uniform costume, grounded high fantasy realism.

## Aurelith as distant presence

Prompt: An ancient elf-like silhouette seen only as a tiny pale figure on a far impossible road above human hills, too distant for expression, cloak still in wind, blue-white light without explanation.

## Cairnkin

Prompt: Stonewise low-cairn people with practical road tools and weathered clothing, distinct from humans but grounded, repairing old stone under rain, no comic exaggeration.

## Vharakul border riders

Prompt: Horned border riders on hardy horses, treaty cords and weathered armor, more humanly practical than monstrous, seen at a market edge with wary villagers.
