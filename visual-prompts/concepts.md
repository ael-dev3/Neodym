# Concept visual prompts

## Thin veil

Prompt: Human village life in the foreground, bread oven, muddy cart, sheep, children, and toll road, with a distant impossible white tower barely visible through rain on the horizon, subtle and unexplained.

## Human war under old silence

Prompt: Two small human war bands lowering banners near a pale forbidden field, mud, fear, wet wool, no grand battle, the ancient boundary making human power look temporary.

## Misread elder light

Prompt: Farmers in a dark field looking up at a faint blue-white light over a far hill, each face showing a different interpretation: fear, prayer, annoyance, curiosity, indifference.

## Human lie using old taboo

Prompt: A bailiff pointing at a forbidden boundary on a crude map while hiding a bloodstained letter under his hand, candlelight, human greed, no elf present.
