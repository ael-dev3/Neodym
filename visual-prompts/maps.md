# Map Image Prompts



## Full world map

Subject: Full world map of Edrath.
Map style: in-world parchment and moon-silver ink reference map.
Orientation: north-up vertical parchment.
Terrain types: mountains, drowned forests, sea reefs, volcanic crowns, mining valleys, human provinces.
Labels: all major regions labeled; use invented fantasy calligraphy that feels readable but not modern.
Borders and icons: distinguish Quiet Concord control, human provinces, rebel routes, other-race homelands, dead zones, trade roads, and Neodym mines with consistent symbols.
Decorative frame: silver archive frame with red thread corner knots.
Mythic symbols: crescent mirror, red thread, Neodym vein, moon shell, root circle, brightstone lamp, Dravyr ember heart where relevant.
Color palette: parchment, ash, black ink, Neodym silver-blue, blood red, rust, pearl, moss green, warm clay.
Level of detail: high, usable as both worldbuilding reference and beautiful in-world artifact.
Artifact type: specify whether it is parchment, etched silver, crystal projection, rebel charcoal, stitched cloth, or carved stone according to the style above.
Negative prompt: avoid satellite maps, modern UI, sci-fi hologram clichés, generic sci-fi, modern screens, obvious robots, futuristic guns, plastic armor, Tolkien-copy elves, generic D&D costumes, random neon cityscapes, anime style unless requested, cheerful cartoon tone, low-detail fantasy tropes, overly clean poster art.
